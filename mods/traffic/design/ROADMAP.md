# sw3-traffic roadmap: activities after worldbuilding

Date: 2026-11-04.

## 0. Scope

This roadmap orders the activity features considered in the 2026-11 research rounds (round 1 ideas A01-A36, round 2 ideas B01-B16, the merge candidate and the community requests answered by a rejection), on top of the built traffic and worldbuilding modules. It says what to build, in which order, in which chapters each feature runs, and how the player first meets it. It does not replace [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md), which tracks implementation, or [proposals/activities-after-worldbuilding.md](proposals/activities-after-worldbuilding.md) ("the proposals"), which holds the evidence, the skeptic reviews and the fixes. Section numbers in parentheses, such as (2b.3), point into the proposals.

## 3. Roadmap

Rules for every wave (proposals 4b):
- Lines: every new entry line goes through `TRF_ArrivalLine` and counts in `arr_max` 3, in the 4b.1 priority order; a dropped line keeps its record for a later entry.
- Markers: at most two area markers of offered, not accepted content per system (R12).
- Dock box: one mod box per docking, one new offer per docking; a declined giver waits one region tick (R10). Intro quests take the box slot or a jobs row as the freight quest does at 450.
- Hail: the dialog hail menu (tune `hail_box` 2, `TRF_Hail.xml` msg00) has one spare provider slot; A05 takes it, and one more provider item would drop that menu to the box chain (`reason=slots`). The shipped default `hail_box` 1 shows one box per option with no slot limit. No other roadmap item adds a hail answer.
- Save: the proposals count 6.8 KB of headroom from the 2026-10-31 host full state (93,194 bytes); the freight quest raised it to 94,376 bytes (freight-quest-spec 3.3) and Wolves Freight step 2 to 95,376 (freight-spec 3.3), so about 4.6 KB is left. The recommended set costs about 935 bytes (4b.2), about 20 % of that.
- Intro quests: `TRF.wq` costs up to 379 bytes at its widest. Six intro quests with their own records would cost about 2.3 KB. This roadmap proposes one shared intro-quest record (the active quest, its step, a seed, one done bit per quest; est. under 100 bytes) and one intro quest active at a time.

Effort: S under a week, M one to three weeks, L a month or more; "+ quest" adds an intro quest of 3-4 steps on built hooks (about S each; the freight quest took five feature commits: three for its steps, a hauler variant and one for its dialogs, letters and mission-list entry, plus review fixes).
