## 1. Summary

- What: three named mercenary crews, taken from the twelve units of the vanilla Mercenary Office, each based in one region. Since the merge they are three of the twelve clans of `12-mercenary-packs.md` in the rival role (`rl` 1), chosen at the region tick by standing and fortune (12 section 6, Roles). They take escort contracts the player leaves open, answer raids the player ignores, keep an FTU escort rating against the player's, hold a grudge, and at a high grudge ambush the player away from stations.
- Unlock: chapter 4 for rumours only (records exist, no flights); chapter 5 (`CurrentQuest`, the guard ends) for competition; chapter 7 (the war opens; the Hunting channel reports units crowding into New Empire space, `x_news.loc:435`) for hostile meetings. Only in regions where a rival is based and only where the strict gate passes.
- Pays: no new pay of its own. It moves existing traffic pay: a rival that answers a raid first halves the player's raid-help pay (8.5); a Wolves lead in a region widens the 8.4 contract offer rule; hostile meetings drop mercenary loot. About 0 early (ch 1-4, off), -2..+49k/h mid, +15..+110k/h late, mostly loot (section 5, recomputed in review).
- Costs: lost contracts and halved raid pay where the rival leads; grudge fights with Rec-row mercenary flights; heat and rating loss if the player shoots first, and every rival ship lost on-screen costs the pack condition, so a shot-up pack breaks and leaves the region; an optional FTU arbitration fee of `1500 x L_w x (g - 5)` (sink).
- Needs: traffic v0.2 contracts, raids, the 8.5 proxy, hail and board (8.2, 8.4, 8.5, S19, S21), v0.2 region cycle and tiers (6.9, 7.12), the W9/escort-AI experiment (engine fact 11); the clan record of feature 12 (the former hire exclusion is now the role field); hooks into 4, 6, 8, 14.

## 2. The player's experience

Worked example, about 8 minutes. Chapter 8, "Free time" in the logbook. Kadi region in Build-up (clan T2). Rival: Black Legion, based in Kadi, rating 7, grudge 3. The Wolves' FTU rating in Kadi is 4. The player jumps into Gaoba (never named in `Quests.xml`, TDR 9.9).

   > "Black Legion, FTU registered."
   > "Freighter, form up on us. The Wolves are busy admiring the view."
   The flight takes station on the freighter. If the player hails it now: "Sorry, Wolves. We're already under escort." The contract option is gone.
2. Two minutes later, a distress line from another trader 1,600 units out (lane raid, 7.6). The player arrives after 2 minutes. Black Legion answered at the 90 s mark and is already fighting. On the player's first counted fire:
   > "Nice of you to show up, Wolves. Half the fee is ours. The FTU keeps count."
   The raid breaks. Screen line: "Raid answered: 3,400 cr. Black Legion answered first, the FTU split the fee." Black Legion's leader: "Good shooting, Wolves. I owe you one. Don't let it go to your head." (grudge 3 -> 1).
3. The player docks at the Gaoba patrol station. After the trouble answer (S19) two screen lines:
   > "FTU escort rating in this sector: Star Wolves 4."
   > "FTU escort rating in this sector: Black Legion 7."

What the player notices later:
- Kadi enters Raids. The player takes four contracts in Kadi over the next hour. Black Legion loses ships in the raids off-screen. At the next board: Wolves 7, Black Legion 5. Traders in Kadi now offer escorts even in calm systems (the lead rule), and the rival snipes less.

A broken crew leaves Kadi, stays away for at least 24 legs and 8 jumps (about 50 minutes), and re-forms weaker in another region under its own name (UC Q9; the former "sometimes under another unit's name" is dropped).

**Exploits and the rule that closes each** (read with the merge: `rp` is `rp_eq = floor(fo / 10)`; the return "at `rp - 3`" is `fo` := max(10, `fo` - 30); the per-rival stamp `r[i].at` is the clan's `met`; the per-system stamp is dropped, the per-clan `met` cooldown closes exploit 6; the bomber flight comes from stage 2; fortune and stage exploits are in `12-mercenary-packs.md` section 5, exploits 22-33):
1. Grudge farming: do jobs to raise `g`, collect ambush loot, repeat. Closed by `rv_fight_cd` 16 legs per rival, `g := 2` after every meeting, `hp -1` per ship lost (a won meeting usually breaks the pack), the break absence of 24 legs and 8 jumps and the return at `rp - 3` in another region, one rival per region, HOSTILE only at `hp >= 2` and the bomber flight only at `hp` 4, and the 50 % retreat rule (a pack leaves when half its ships are dead, so a full wipe needs a chase).
2. Murdering rival flights for mercenary loot without waiting for a grudge (review: the original design had no pack cost for this, so every SNIPE or ANSWER flight could be farmed at up to the late farm rate). Closed by `hp -1` per ship lost (a wiped flight breaks the pack), heat +1 per counted attack and per kill (traffic tiers then refuse trade and cap the trader tier, 7.8), `w` -2 per kill, and after 2 rival kills in a visit no rival action spawns for the rest of it. Counted fire and kills by a hired feature 12 pack count as the player's.
3. Save/reload to re-roll a snipe, an answer or an ambush, or to cancel a fee split. Closed by deterministic rolls per (system, rival, window), cooldown stamps written at spawn, live state void on load (8.4 rule), and the split decided by the first counted fire, which is recorded at the event.
