## 1. Summary

- What: the twelve mercenary units the game ships with names, bios, pilot templates and two rival blocs (`G/LocData/English/m_recruits.loc:8-52`, `G/Scripts/include/RandomFmerc.script:9-11`, `G/Game/Pilots.xml:4479`) but never spawns (all 100 `RegisterNaemnik` lines in 94 `Locations/*/activate.script` are commented out) appear as packs that jump in where the regional cycle has work, offer to escort the Wolves for the visit, and remember how they were treated.
- Roles and growth (UC A, C, D): one persistent record per clan (`TRF.mc`, section 6) with one role at a time: free (hireable, this spec), rival (the behaviour of `13-rival-packs.md`, chosen by standing and fortune) or broken (absent; re-forms under its own name). A fortune `fo` 0..99 earned from regional work sets a stage 1-4 (Crew, Squadron, Flagship, Mothership): a young clan fields fighters, a veteran clan a Cavalier corvette (stage 3) or the vanilla "Mercenary mothership" `Base_mercenary_1` (stage 4) with a fighter wing escorting it. NPC carriers cannot launch or recover fighters through any native (NC:9, :15-16) [V], so the wing is a separate flight that escorts the big ship. Big ships never escort the player and are never hired.
- Beyond the visit (P D.1): bookings by captains and clans, revenge flights, hunts after stash theft or betrayal, warnings and outbids, debt markers, calls paid in standing, ace transfer (section 6 "Options beyond the visit"; P M1-M4, M7, M12, M14, M15).
- When: chapter 5 or later (the starting-region guard has ended, M20), in a system of a region where a clan is working: mostly Build-up and Raids phases or with a faction sweep scheduled. Never by player level.
- Pay: none. It is a sink. Fee `700 x L_w x tier_k x (1 + 0.05 x pro) x d_k` times `mc_size_k` by stage, capped at `pay_cap`, plus a prize share of `25 x L_w^2 x d_k` per ship of a group the pack is at war with that dies while the pack flies (about 25-40 % of that ship's loot, uncapped), plus blood money of 4 x the share per pack ship lost. About 10-20 % of the stage's farm rate when used (section 5).
- Cost to the player: credits, and standing per clan (`rel` -3..3). Abandoning a fighting pack, failing to pay or shooting it costs `rel` and can blacklist the Wolves at the Exchange. Heat 5+ raises the fee, heat 7+ closes hire.
- Needs: traffic v0.2 regions, phases, clan tiers and the 8.5 fire proxy (6.9, 7.12, 8.5), v1 sweep schedule (7.13), strict gate and deny list (9.8 S4, 9.9), caps (6.7); shares the escort slot with #9; the #4 wing pilot hook, #7 `src` 5 and the stash grudge hook (`S/trf_stash.script:1056`, `:1060` in the working tree, TR F.8), the #6 pod path; checks EM.0-EM.6 (P C.1) and EMS.1-EMS.6 (section 8). The former step 2 (standing pack across jumps, which needed the snapshot and `SetHP` answers, WB engine facts 15, 22) is replaced by calls paid in standing (P M4, section 9).
- Black Legion (section 11): the elite clan. Same power rule as every clan; a stage floor (3 from chapter 5, 4 from chapter 7) and the reserved stage 4 slot keep it at the top. Legend lines from chapter 1, first flight in chapter 5, hire only through a gate from chapter 7, an elite HOSTILE challenge; beating it is recorded once as an achievement, and the Legion withdraws for 60 jumps and re-forms one stage lower.
- Names (`../proposals/merc-pilot-names.md`, added 2026-10-24): each clan's leader and four aces have names, two per slot (placeholders). With the opt-in data merge `install.sh --merc-names` (cfg `mc_names` 2) the engine shows them as pilot callsigns; without it they appear only in the mod's lines [I until EN.1].
- Default, pending user confirmation: UC Q1 (merge), Q3 (stage hulls), Q4 (big ships never escort or hire); P Q10 (step 2 replaced by M4).

## 2. The player's experience

Worked example, about 7 minutes. Chapter 9, the logbook shows "Free time". The Kadi region is in Raids, its clan at T3 ("Black Comets", a placeholder for the authored clan name, TDR 5.12). Two units work Kadi this cycle: the Star Dragoons (bloc 2) and the Cimmerians (bloc 1). The player has never hired either (`rel` 0). Gaoba (Kadi region, never named in `Quests.xml`, TDR 9.9) has `pro` 4; its vanilla trader level is 13 (`G/Scripts/Locations/Gaoba/activate.script:217`), so `L_w` is about 10 (inference from `sys_base`, TDR 5.1).

1. The player jumps in from Heos. Traffic's arrival line runs first: "Gaoba: lanes in trouble, Black Comets raiding."
2. 50 s later the XK-10 portal flares. Four fighters come through (`Rec_Fighters` row 12 on NORMAL: Stormcrow, Cleaner, EvilEye, Raptor, `G/Scripts/include/Create_flight_2.script:710`), move 150 units off the portal mouth and wait. A nav marker reads "Mercenaries". Radio from the leader:
   > "Star Dragoons, working the Kadi lanes. Raiders giving you grief, Wolves? We're parked by the XK-10 gate."
3. The player flies over. At 70 units:
   > "Four guns, one price: 12,600. Pirates we shoot. Navy we don't. Plus 2,500 a head for every pirate that falls while we fly, and blood money for any of ours that falls."
   12 s later a box, caption "Mercenaries" (vanilla `#UI_Fmerc_confirm`, `G/LocData/English/ui_windows.loc:54`; vanilla's own box text is `#UI_Fmerc_join1`, `:55`, used by `RandomFmerc.script:244-245`):
   > "Star Dragoons will fly with you in this system for 12600 credits, plus 2500 per pirate downed. Hire them?"
4. OK. Screen log "Mercenaries have joined you." (vanilla `#UI_Fmerc_joined`, `ui_windows.loc:57`). Leader: `"Contract's on. We stay on your six till you jump or ten minutes run out."` They take up escort positions.
5. A staged raid (TDR 7.6, S21) calls for help on the Lidau lane. The player's wing opens fire on the raiders; the moment that fire counts (8.5 proxy), the pack is set at war with that raid group: `"Got them. Dragoons, break and engage!"` Two raid ships fall to the pack, one to the player's wing; the raid leader breaks off at 30 % (T3 rule, TDR 7.12). The trader thanks the Wolves and traffic pays raid help (8.5) at half rate, because the player group killed a raider while a pack was hired (section 5, rule 6).
6. An NESF response flight passes on the way to an unrelated fight. The pack ignores it. If it ever fires on the Wolves: `"That's navy, boss. Not in the contract."`
7. Ten game minutes after the hire (about 75 s of real time at 8x), while nothing is shooting, the leader asks: `"Time's up, Wolves. Another ten minutes for 9,500?"` with Extend and Settle up [user decision 2026-10-25: a hire is time based only and follows the player through portals; the extension costs `merc_ext_k` x fee]. The player settles: the settlement dialog lists the share and blood money, Pay. `"Good hunting today."` Screen log: "Star Dragoons: prize share for 3 pirates, 7500 credits." The player picked up the loot of all three (about 21k of sale value at mid stage, brief 1), so the hire cost about 20k and returned about 21k plus raid help: the gain is the fight survived and the time saved, not credits. The pack flies to the XK-10 portal and leaves.

What the player notices later:
- Next meeting with the Star Dragoons anywhere in their current region (`rel` 1): `"The Wolves! Gaoba was clean work. Friends' rate: 11,300."`
- If the player had jumped out while the pack was still fighting: `"You left us hanging in Gaoba, Wolves. Price went up."` (`rel` -2, fee +10 %); at `rel` -2 or lower: `"We don't fly for the Wolves. Try the Cimmerians, if they'll have you."` and the box never opens.
- If two Dragoons had died: blood money 2 x 10,000 at contract end, and next time `"We buried two in Gaoba. Rate's up till the wake's over."` (grief premium, decays).
- Six legs later Kadi is in Bust. No packs appear in Gaoba. The Gaoba patrol board (S19 wrapper) adds one line: "Mercenaries for hire: Star Dragoons in the Dosis lanes." The units follow the work.
- If the player had shot the pack: `"Turncoat! Dragoons, weapons free on the carrier!"`, the pack fights back, and on the next entry: "The Mercenary Exchange has struck the Wolves off its books." No unit offers anything for 30 jumps (60 after a second offence). The pack breaks off once half of it is down, so the player never gets the whole pack's loot.
- (P D.2) A patrol board in a region where a captain or clan is booking: "Mercenary Exchange: the Black Comets are hiring guns.", or "... want the Wolves' hide." after the player cracked that clan's cache (M2).
- (P M14) A scout of a booked unit arrives, holds at 1,500 units with a nav marker and says "Wolves. Rourke says hello. Next jump, we come for you."; a later entry in that region brings the hunt.
- (P M1, M14) A revenge or hunt flight speaks a reason line read from the record: "You left us hanging in Sunji, Wolves. Rourke pays on time." or "You cracked the Comets' rock, Wolves. They pay well for that."
- (P M3) A loyal unit warns ("Rourke tried to buy us. We said no. He's working the Sunji lanes.") and the next dock in the region offers one outbid box ("Buy the Cimmerians off Rourke's book through the Dragoons: 7,600?").
- (P M15) After the player picks up a pack pilot's pod and pays the contract in full: "You pulled Harlan out. The Dragoons owe the Wolves one."
- (UC D.7) A clan that grew arrives with a new hull: "New hull, Wolves. Business is good."; at stage 4 the signature says "Golden Griffins mothership on station. Our birds are out." and the board line adds "(mothership)". UC section H is a full timeline of one clan from crew to mothership and back.

## 3. Narrative fit

**Lore.** The handbook calls mercenaries the pirates' "worst enemy" and "the only convincing guarantee of a trader's safe journey" (`G/LocData/English/x_news.loc:25`). The Hunting channel tracks the brotherhood across the war: teams destroying gangs in Debris 1, XB-17, XT-26 and Valencia (`x_news.loc:375`, posted by `G/Scripts/Quests/mission_5_1/Mission_USS_Debris4_2.script`), bar fights between rival units (`:390`), the MSF demand that units register or be treated as pirates (`:411`, posted at the end of the chapter 6 convoys, `mission_6c_1/Convoy1_El_ariash.script:39`), the move to NESF space (`:435`, chapter 7), "clear outta" MSF systems (`:537`, chapter 12), MSF portal defences killing teams (`:558`, chapter 13), and after peace the call to X297 against the Berserks (`:603`, chapters 16-19). The twelve units are the ones the merc office lists (`G/Game/Recruits.xml` `RecruitInfo` images = `_Fmerc_names`). The developers placed some of them in specific systems before commenting the calls out (table in 6); this design uses those placements as home regions.

**First appearance: chapter 5.** Chapters 1-3 forbid detours (`__crash_Freedom`, `G/Scripts/include/OutOfMission.script:21`) and the Hero flies a transport without hangar until chapter 4; an escort pack for a cargo hauler would make the opening easier than the story intends. Chapter 5 is the first chapter whose news names merc teams at work, and the guard (M20) ends there.

**Story jumps are never abandonment.** `TRF_BeforeJump` runs from both wrapped jump natives, `TeleportToLocation` and `CustomTeleportate` (`mods/traffic/Data/Scripts/Mods/Traffic/traffic.script:228-236`), and cannot tell who called them. So a jump counts as abandonment only when no mission is active (`GetPlayerQuestState() ~= TRUE`, TDR 9.9 fallback) and the destination is not on the deny list; any other jump keeps the hire: the wing follows the player (section 6 End of a hire) [user decision 2026-10-25]. A jump the wing cannot follow (a mission active, a deny-listed destination) ends the hire as completed: the leader asks to settle before it (section 5 Settlement); a jump taken without settling makes the amount `owe`, no `rel` loss.

**Traffic fiction used.** Regions and phases (6.9) decide where units work; the clan tier (7.12) sets the pack's strength and price, because a unit charges for the danger it meets; sweeps (7.13) draw units in, as the Exchange's "Big Sweep" campaign did (`x_news.loc:309`); the FTU stays the traders' union and pays its own contracts (8.4), while packs sell protection to carriers. Units in the free role are not FTU and never escort traders; the rival role does (`13-rival-packs.md` SNIPE).

## 4. Progression fit

**Unlock.** Chapter >= 5 and at least one unit present in the current region (presence follows the cycle, 6). Nothing reads `GetPlayerLevel`.

**Scaling.** Strength (UC D.1) [new]: `pw = clamp(sys_base(s) + off(T_eff(s)) + mc_st_off[sg] - (4 - hp), 1, 20)` with the 7.12 offsets (T1 -3, T2 0, T3 +2, T4 +3), `mc_st_off` -2 / -1 / 0 / +1 by stage `sg`, and `hp` 0..4 the clan's condition. It replaces the former fixed -1 of this spec (one power step below the raids) and 13's `floor(rp/3)`, so strength follows the stage and the world, not the player. Pack kills of pirates change the world through traffic's normal any-killer rules: clan score -2 per flight within `pp_visit_loss_cap` (-6 per visit per clan, TDR 7.12 table at line 902), `dis` -1 (7.10, 7.12). Off-screen presence has no effect on clan score, phase or faction state, so packs cannot push a region or a clan anywhere by themselves. Bookings, grudges and hunt states are counters (W1) and change no clan score, phase or faction value (P D.2); fortune and stage are the clan's own counters and add no counterforce to the cycle (UC D.6). Timers that would favour the player when shortened (blacklist, negative `rel` recovery, `hp` recovery, `bk`, debt expiry, re-form, big-ship cooldowns) count jumps on the shared clock `TRF.clock.j` (+1 in `TRF_State_BeforeJump`, read by `TRF_Jumps()`, `S/trf_state.script:1013-1021` [V]; `00-economy-and-progression.md` section 6, row "Jump counters"), so 8x idling does not clear them. The earlier name `TRF.mj` of this spec is historical and not used (P H (c)). Pack fire is not player-group fire, so it earns no raid help (8.5), no sweep part (8.10), no captain claim (#4) and no visit credits (7.11). Heat feeds back: at heat 5-6 the fee is x1.5 ("We guard freighters, we don't hunt them. Costs extra."), at heat >= 7 every unit refuses. FTU `help >= 15` gives -10 % ("FTU vouches for you"). `rel` buys concrete things: price (-10 % per point above 0), the friendly opening line, a larger weight when two units share a region (weight `rel + 4`), the lane report at `rel >= 1`, warnings and the outbid, calls and the ace transfer at `rel >= 2` (P D.2). A clan with `rel >= 1` is never chosen as a rival (section 6, rival candidate rule).

**By stage.** Chapters 1-4 get nothing by design (guard, section 3); their low-risk income comes from wave 1 features. Early (ch 5-6, `L_w` 4-5, T1-T2): two-ship packs for about 3,000, enough to keep a thin first wing alive in Debris. Mid (ch 7-12, `L_w` 8-11, T2-T3): four-ship packs for 9-14k that let a mid wing answer T3 raids and take join-the-sweep contracts. Late (ch 13-20, `L_w` 14-16, T3-T4): four-ship Puma/Panthera packs on NORMAL (six on HARD) at 22-30k plus a share of about 4,900-6,400 per pirate ship (`L_w` 14-16); calls paid in standing (P M4) replace the former step 2 standing contract. From chapter 7 veteran clans field a corvette (stage 3) or a mothership (stage 4); the wing is hired, the big ship never is.

**Why it cannot be farmed.** One pack per entry; a clan meets the player at most once per `merc_cd` (4) legs; the appearance roll and the clan pick are deterministic per (seed, leg, system), so reloading gives the same answer; a hire lasts `merc_hire_s` (600 game seconds, saved as remaining time) across jumps, longer only through paid extensions [user decision 2026-10-25]; the share takes about a third of the loot of every ship that dies while the pack flies, so pack-assisted farming nets less per ship than solo farming; lost pack ships cost more blood money than they drop; pack kills pay the player nothing; the pirate supply per visit stays bounded by traffic (one staged raid per entry, `raid_live_cap`, `dis` gates). Killing a pack yields at most half a pack's loot, closes all packs for 30-90 jumps; merc pilots pay no bounty (`RecruitPilot_*` are in none of the `CostOfHead` tables, `G/Scripts/include/CostOfPirates.script:28-69`). Section 5 lists each loop with its rule. The options beyond the visit add their own closing rules (P D.2): bookings are phase-bound and deterministic, retainer and outbid credit apply to the same unit in the same phase, one report per 28 legs, one hunt per booking with the grudge ending with the phase, markers are single-use and expire after 60 jumps, calls cost standing and are 8 jumps apart, the transfer is once per clan. Fortune and stages add UC F rows 1-12 (section 5, exploits 22-33).

**Why nothing settles.** Presence moves with the region phases at every region tick, at most two units per region, so a region in Quiet has none and a region in Raids attracts them; `rel`, `hp` and `bk` recover or decay; the blacklist expires; an unpaid debt is written off after 60 jumps; no hired pack stays past the visit. Fortune follows phase income minus upkeep by stage (UC C.3): a clan that works Build-up and Raids regions climbs to stage 3 in about one cycle, stage 4 costs more upkeep (2 per tick) than a worked region pays (+2), so a mothership needs events on top and is lost in a Bust, after losses or when no region within reach has work; at most `mc_ms_max` 2 clans are at stage 4 map-wide. Rival slots refill from other candidates, and a broken clan re-forms elsewhere. The former step 2 standing pack and its lapse rules are removed (section 9).

Formulas (traffic names from TDR 8.4, 11; `L_w` and `d_k` from the progression brief):

At `pro` 3 this equals the #9 faction escort `800 x L_w x tier_k x d_k` (`09-faction-ladder.md:10`), so both escorts cost the same and differ in access (rank 7 for #9) and strength (faction rung vs clan tier).

- Blood money: `bm = c_blood x sh` per pack ship lost, `c_blood` 4 (2,400 / 10,000 / 25,600), above the sale value of what a pack ship drops (inference: one power step below the raid, so below `v`; E12.10).

### 11.3 Hire

Gate [new]. ### 11.5 Beating the Legion

**What counts as beaten** [new]. All of:
1. A HOSTILE meeting of the rival role in which the Legion fields its flagship and the group war was set by the Legion's HOSTILE action (13 section 6), not by counted player fire on a non-hostile Legion ship.
2. The meeting ends by the Legion's break-off (section 11.4): (a) the flagship dies, or (b) the Legion leaves with 4 of 6 wing ships dead or the flagship below 25 % HP.
3. Counted player-group fire (8.5 proxy, `T_deliberate`) on the flagship for (a), and for (b) on the flagship or on at least 3 of the dead wing ships.
4. No hired pack and no called unit flew in that visit (`TRF.mc.h` and `TRF.mc.r` empty for the visit), because the engine reports no killer (UC D.5) and a third party's kill must not count.
5. The result is written to `TRF.mc` at the event (`died` or break-off), as every loss is (UC C.7).

**What does not count:** kills by third parties off screen (off-screen results cost `hp` only and stop at `hp` 2); kills while a hired pack or a called unit is present; shooting a hired Legion or any non-hostile Legion ship (betrayal under section 4: `rel` := -3, bloc-mates -2, `bk` +1, blacklist, heat); booked and hunt flights (no flagship); a HOSTILE meeting ended by the station rule or by a wind-down; a reload, since a load before the event replays the same spawn from the saved record and fixed hash and a load after it keeps the result.

**Reward, on the first beating only** (`dj1` 0 before) [new]:
- Personal record: `dj1` := `TRF.clock.j`, `dc1` := chapter, `djl` := `TRF.clock.j`, `bt` +1 (section 6 Saved state).
- Title: from then on the patrol board line (S19, through `TRF_DockLine`, `S/trf_plumb.script:300-310`) shows "Deeds: the Star Wolves broke the Black Legion (first in chapter <dc1>, beaten <bt>).", and every clan's opening line at `rel >= 0` has a title variant ("The Wolves who broke the Legion. Same price, more respect."). Hunting post with `news_mode` 2, once.
- Standing, once: `rel` +1, at most 2, with every bloc 1 clan (the Legion's rivals at the Exchange); no change for bloc 2. The discount follows from that `rel` through `disc` (section 5); no separate discount factor exists.
- Ace transfer unlocked: at the first non-hostile meeting with the re-formed Legion at `rel >= -1` and `bk` 0, one Legion ace with his hull is offered without the 3-hire requirement and without the 1.5 premium: price `max(office cost, resale + 10 %)`, capped at `pay_cap`, once per campaign (`rc`), gated by EM.3 like P M7.

A later beating counts for the record again (`bt` +1, `djl`, board line), with no second reward.

**Consequence for the Legion: withdrawal and re-formation** [new]. Nobody wins and no clan is removed (R2, UC Q6). After any beating the Legion withdraws: `rl` := 2 (absent, its rival slot is freed), re-form at `legs >= cd` with `cd` := legs + `rv_cd_legs` 24 and `TRF.clock.j >= cj` with `cj` := `TRF.clock.j` + `mc_el_wd` 60 jumps (1.5 x the 40-jump mothership cooldown, about 4-5 hours at 12-15 jumps per hour [I]). ### 11.8 Station, later (step 3) [IDEA]

A Legion outpost in its home region, built on the pirate-base machinery (`../v1-spec.md` section 9, `S/trf_base.script`). Idea only; nothing below is specified for building.

What the files support:
- Hull. `Base_mercenary_1` ("Mercenary mothership", BigShip, 2,000 base HP, `SD:4186-4192`, `CX:821`) can be placed with `CreateCarcass` at a fixed site; the pirate-base research names a big-ship base by `CreateCarcass` as a working fallback (`../research/pirate-bases.md:11`). It is a ship class, and the location snapshot keeps only stations, space carcasses and portals (`../research/pirate-bases.md:133`), so the outpost must be re-created on every entry, like a base garrison, with its HP band from the record (`SetHP`, E0.20).
- Site. `llanovar1` or `llanovar2` (never named, `trader=1`, `S/map.script:823`, `:833`), placed by the ring rule of `../v1-spec.md` 9.1. Neither is on the pirate-base allow-list (`dg` 0, no `ba`), so the outpost needs its own allow-list entry [new].
- Offer through the existing meeting trigger, not a dock: the 150-unit stage 4 meeting trigger on the mothership (section 6 Spawn) opens the box chain: hire box (section 11.3 gate), the ace transfer (11.5), a rumour line and the deeds and rating lines (11.5, 13 `w`). The scripted dock functions work on any object with a position (`CreatePirateStationDocking` with `boolUseRadius` TRUE, `../research/pirate-bases.md:103`), but they open `TradeStationDlg(shop)` on a named engine shop.
- When: only while the Legion is active, at stage 4, with `at` = Torr and no rival action that visit; the outpost is the Legion's own mothership parked at home, so it uses the reserved stage 4 slot and adds no second big ship.

Out of scope: a real dockable trade station with its own shop. The only shops the files document are vanilla named shops (the contraband `pirateN` shops are shared with vanilla stations and must not be destroyed, `../research/pirate-bases.md:123`), a mod row in a shop window is unverified (`../mod-research/adding-ships.md:417`, O4), and a persistent station needs a `Station` object. The one vanilla merc station model, `Merc_Base` (`Station`, 300,000 HP, `CX:7796`, used at `G/Scripts/Locations/Kassilia/location.script:80`), shows the name "Mercenary base Sherwood" (`G/LocData/English/s_carcasses.loc:328`), which belongs to Kassilia. A Legion station therefore needs a new map object and shop, which no research note covers.

Risks: a parked big ship in a never-named system counts against `cap_ships` and the governor on every entry (EMS.4); shooting it is betrayal; it shares `live.box` with station boxes; `Base_mercenary_1` is a story hull of missions 3 and 4.1 (exploit 33), so it never spawns while a quest that names it is in process.

Checks (idea only): EL.O1 the parked mothership holds its site at 1x and 8x after `Move` and `Stop`; EL.O2 the 150-unit meeting trigger re-arms after each box chain; EL.O3 re-creation on entry with `SetHP` per band and no duplicate after a load.

Default, pending user confirmation: EL Q7 (outpost as an idea only), EL Q8 (no trade station without a new map object).
