### 2.1 Decisions for the owner

The recommendation is the default above; the user's standing rule makes each recommendation the built default.

1. Module (brief). Recommended: a new module `trf_survey` (prefix `Sy`), as every roadmap item. trf_warn would need saved state (bits, chart blocks), a tick, a dock box, a quest and a generator; its spec keeps it a list with lines. Alternative: extend trf_warn (`TRF.wn` grows by the same 73 bytes, one module less in `extra_count`).
2. Reading payoff (P 2.6). Recommended: the survey fee (`sy_fee_k` 5 % of `TRF_PayN` per weight point: 450-1,350 cr at `TRF_PayN` 9,000, which `L_w` 8, tier 2 and prosperity 5 give) plus one information line (6.3). Alternative A: the information line only (`sy_fee_k` 0). Alternative B: a small find through `PlaceModule` after proposals 6 item 1 passes (not specified here).
3. XP (P 6 item 15). Recommended: no XP. Offline answer: `CalculateExpPoints` (`G/Scripts/include/LevelUp.script:31-34`) applies x0.1 to kill XP when `GetPlayerQuestState() ~= TRUE`; a reading is no kill, so the only XP path is `AddPlayerExperience`, which is B02's channel and waits on roadmap decision 1. Alternative: once decision 1 is approved, register the hand-in of 7.3 as a sixth B02 settlement kind (`TRF_Settle("survey", idx, 0, "sy")`, debrief-spec D1 extension).
4. Hold. Recommended: 250 units, 20 s of game time, reset on leaving, paused by hold flags. Alternative: 150 units and 10 s.
5. Markers. Recommended: job markers for the three quest rows only; the survey after the quest has none, only the near line at 800 units. Alternative A: no marker at all (the pitch). Alternative B: survey rows marked as offer owner `survey` (counts in `mk_offer` 2, so a signal and a cache would block it).
6. Offer place. Recommended: the charter office (`TRF_Ch_Office()` answers: chapter 5-18, help 3, the strict gate "charter"), as DB. Alternative: any trade dock from chapter 5 without the charter gate.
7. Dialog windows. Recommended: none in this step: box, mission card or Office row for the offer, a dock line for the hand-in. Alternative: `TRF_Sy1.xml` (offer) and `TRF_Sy2.xml` (hand-in) with the FTU clerk `TRF_WqP_clerk`, as `TRF_Db1/2.xml` (about one feature commit more).
8. Chart sale. Recommended: automatic at the first trade dock outside the region (at most `sy_sell_max` 2 per docking). Alternative: a sell box (takes the dock's one box slot; needs a decline wait).
9. Chart value. Recommended: the 8.2 formula (`sy_chart_k` 8 % per region weight point: Dosis 12,960 cr fresh at `PayN` 9,000; -15 % per phase step, floor 25 %, 40 legs counts as 5 steps). Alternative: a flat value per region with no decay.
10. Thresholds. Recommended: 30 and 60 points, +10 % each. Alternative: none (no line, no bonus).
11. Landmark table (data note 2). Recommended: the 4.1 rules: 46 rows. Alternative A: stash slot distance 650 (reading radius plus 400): Beta Lethera 3 (2 rows), Altair and XB-13 lose their rows and the region caps refill Cadi and Golconda from other systems (still 46). Alternative B: 10 rows per region instead of 8 (54).
12. Regions without a card. Recommended: accept that XT-27 (2 rows: XT-81, XT-93) never gets a card and Gefest (3 rows, one a random portal) only from chapter 8; their rows count once the survey is open. Alternative: a 250-unit dock rule and a 200-unit hostile rule, which give XT-26's station ruin a point 300 units from the ruin (XT-27 gets 3 rows; the point sits nearer a trade station and a pirate spawn than every other row).
13. Save (3.5): 73 bytes worst case against the roadmap's 58 and the brief's 80. Accept.
14. Wolves' name. Recommended: the sale line plus ring kind 14 told by harbour news. Alternative: the sale line only (no trf_tips edit).
15. Survey gate. Recommended: the survey opens with the quest's done bit (`sy_gate` 1). Alternative `sy_gate` 0: open from chapter 5 for every player, no quest offered.

## 3. Data model

### 3.1 Saved string (`TRF.sy`, version stays 4; created at the first reading, repaired by `TRF_Sy_Fix`)

`TRF.sy = "<bits>/<block 1>/<block 2>/.../<block n>"`, `n = TRF_RgMap().n` (8). nil when no bit is set and every block is empty. No other saved field of trf_survey; the quest lives in `TRF.iq` (3.3).

### 3.2 Fields

| Part | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `bits` | `ceil(N / 4)` characters of `0123456789abcdef`, `N = getn(TRF_SyData())` (46; at most 60, so at most 15) | row `id` is read when bit `j = mod(id - 1, 4)` of character `floor((id - 1) / 4) + 1` is set (`TRF_SyBitOf`, `TRF_SySetOf`; arithmetic with `floor`, `mod` and `2^j`, no bit operators in Lua 4) |
| block `b` | `""` | region slot `b` has no complete chart |
| | `"<p><ggg>"` (4 digits) | complete, not sold: `p` 0..5 the region's phase at completion, `ggg` leg mod 1000 of completion; `age = mod(mod(legs, 1000) - ggg + 1000, 1000)` (valid while `sy_chart_old` <= 200) |
| | `"s"` | sold (once per region, kept for good) |

Rules:
- SD1 A bit is set once, at the reading (6.3); nothing clears it except `TRF_Sy_Fix` for ids above `N`.
- SD2 Ids are the row order of the generated table (4.1). A generator change that adds or removes a row moves the later ids; such a change needs a migration step in `TRF_Sy_Fix` (none for the first build), and `gen_survey.py` prints `ids changed` against the block it replaces.
- SD3 The chart block is written at the reading that completes the region (6.4) and set to `s` at the sale (8.1).

### 3.3 Quest record (`TRF.iq`, quest id 4, TE 3.1)

| Digit field | Value |
|---|---|
| `q` | 4 (`ct`) |
| `s` | 1 step 10 (three readings), 2 step 20 (hand-in) |
| `t` | id of the first named row (1..60) |
| `sd` | `id2 x 100 + id3` (the second and third named rows; at most 6060 < 10^4) |
| `c` | read mask 0..7 (bit 1 the `t` row, 2 `id2`, 4 `id3`); 7 at step 20 |

`TRF_IqTake(4, id1, id2 x 100 + id3)` at the accept, `TRF_IqSet(4, s, t, c)` at each count and the step change, `TRF_IqEnd(4, "done")` at the hand-in (done bit 8; no reward bits). The region is the slot of the three rows. `TRF_IqOwners()` gains index 4 = `"TRF_Sy_IqLapse"`.

Quest repair (`TRF_Sy_Fix`, after `TRF_Pl_Fix` has repaired `TRF.iq`): with `q` 4: `s` 1 or 2; `t`, `floor(sd / 100)` and `mod(sd, 100)` each 1..`N`, distinct, of one region; `c` 0..7; `s` 1 with `c` 7 -> `TRF_IqSet(4, 2, t, 7)`; `s` 2 with `c ~= 7` -> `c` 7; anything else `TRF_IqEnd(4, "dropped")` with `event=fix field=quest result=dropped reason=<field>`; done bit 8 with an active `q` 4 -> dropped.

### 3.4 Visit state (chunk local `SY`, never saved, rebuilt by every MapInitialization)

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `lm` | the rows of the current system that are open now (6.1), each `{id, r}`; empty table: the tick returns at once |
| `h[id]` | hold seconds of game time |
| `nr[id]`, `hs[id]` | 1 = near line / hold line said this visit |
| `ok` | the cached result of R1-R6 of the last 1 s step |
| `q` | visit latch: `TRF_Safe_QState()` seen this visit |
| `pr[reason]` | 1 = this skip reason logged this visit |
| `mk` | 1 = the quest markers of this system are held; `mq` 1 after a refusal (quiet retry, the tenth argument of `TRF_MarkerAdd`) |
| `v.dec`, `offer` | an offer declined this visit; the open offer `{sys, g, ids}` |
| `tc` | the clamped tune table and the `TRF_Tune` table it was built from |
| `rows` | `TRF_SyData()` and the per-region index (row ids by slot, weights `W_r`), built once per visit |

The last Job log text sits in a function global `TRF_SyKeepThunk` (as `TRF_DbKeepThunk`, DB 3.2), so an unchanged entry is not rewritten; a load drops it and the first trade dock after it rewrites once. No handle in `TRF`.

### 3.5 Size (host grammar `HOST_SaveSize`: string 8 + n, string key 8 + n)

| Item | Bytes |
|---|---|
| key `sy` | 10 |
| string header | 8 |
| bits at `N` 60 / 46 | 15 / 12 |
| separators (bits plus 8 blocks) | 8 |
| 8 complete unsold charts | 32 |
| worst case (60 rows) | 10 + 8 + 15 + 8 + 32 = 73 |
| worst case with the generated 46 rows | 70 |
| typical (20 rows read, one chart unsold, one sold) | 10 + 8 + 12 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 43 |
| quest 4 in `TRF.iq` | 0 extra: the four numbers are counted at their widest (TE 3.4) |
| ring kind 14 | 0 extra: `TRF.ti` stays bounded at 409 bytes by `ti_ring` |
| full state `v1-n` | 97,663 + 73 = 97,736 of 100,000 |

The roadmap's 58 bytes (P 4b.2: a 60-bit set of about 10 bytes plus 4 chart records of about 12) count raw fields; the key and the eight region blocks cost the difference. Count caps: one string, `N` <= 60 bits, 8 blocks of at most 4 characters.

## 4. Landmarks

### 4.1 Table (`TRF_SyData()`, generated by `tools/gen_survey.py`)

A block in `S/trf_survey.script` between `-- BEGIN gen_survey.py (do not edit by hand)` and `-- END gen_survey.py`. `python3 -I mods/traffic/tools/gen_survey.py [--game P] [--write | --check]`, read-only on the game; imports `gen_map` (parsers `scan`, `split_args`, `eval_vec`, `parse_location`) and `gen_stash` (`collect`, `slots`). Row `{s="xt20", k="hk", w=3, x=-687, y=0, z=382, p=1}` (`p` the index of the nearest managed portal in the map entry's `portals`, for the place phrase). Order (= the id): region slot, map idx, kind order `rp gr sr hk wf cs`, x, z.

- Systems: every `map.script` system with a region slot, except the `k="kill"` rows of trf_warn's list (parsed from the `-- BEGIN warn list` block: gideon, eridan, xt86, vulcan, xt24, zebos), fronn and the authored `m = 1` deny entries of `TRF_DenyList` (elio, x297, gefest), and nk (New Kyoto, 00 section 4).
- Objects: top-level `CreateCarcass`, `CreateStation`, `CreateTradeStation`, `CreateAsteroid` calls of `location.script` with a statically resolved position; nothing from activate.script, functions.script or quest scripts.
- Kinds:
  - `rp` random portal: the object assigned to `RAND_PORTAL` (`CreateCarcass("unknown", xyz_rand_portal, ...)`); the reading point is the portal itself.
  - `gr` gate: templates matching `portal_destro|portal_broken|^prec_portal$`, single linkage at 300, centroid.
  - wrecks: `CreateCarcass` templates matching `destroyed|destroed|garbage|crushes|^crash_site$|^broken_station$` (scan-tooling-spec GW1) without the gate ones, single linkage at 250, mass-weighted centroid (mass from `Carcasses.xml`, mass 0 -> `min(1000000, 20 x hit_points)`, GW3). A cluster with a station-ruin template (`Broken_station`, `p_station_destroyed`, `p_station2_destroyed`, `Berserk_station_destroyed`, `Mining_station_destroyed`, `Station_triada_destroyed`, `CargoModule_destroyed`) is `sr`; else one with a capital hull (`MS<n>[F]_destroyed`, `LOS<n>_destroyed`, `linkor_destroyed_<n>`, `Alien_dread_destroyed`, `Super_Chimera_destroyed`, `Satan_destroyed`, `pirat_mothership_arba_destroyed`) is `hk`; else a cluster of at least 3 is `wf`; smaller clusters are dropped.
  - `cs` closed stations: `CreateStation` calls >= 120 from every `map.script` station, without `pirat`, `TradeStation`, `PStation` in the template and not a container or prop (`_inv`, `radiopod`, `buoy`, `logo`, `NULL`, `PREC_`, `Rock_hideout`); single linkage at 450; clusters of at least 2.
- Weight `w`: `rp` 3, `gr` 3, `sr` 2, `hk` 2 with a cluster mass >= 1,000,000 else 1, `wf` 1, `cs` 1; +1 in a dead-end system (one managed portal without `cond` and no `RAND_PORTAL`); at most 3.
- Reading point: `rp` the portal position; the others the landmark position, then rings of 150, 220 and 300 units at 8 bearings (45 degrees, the landmark's `y`); the first point that is:
  - >= 400 from every `map.script` station and every pirate station (`pirat` in a `CreateStation` template) (`dock`);
  - >= 300 from every managed portal (`portal`);
  - >= 400 from every stash slot of `TRF_ShSlotData()` (`slot`, D7);
  - >= 300 from every statically resolved `NewPirateFlight` and `NewBerserkFlight` position of activate.script (`hostile`);
  - >= 60 from every asteroid (`ast`) and >= 100 from every other static object (`object`); `rp` points skip both;
  - >= 400 from the system's `RAND_PORTAL` (`rp`, D5);
  - >= 300 from vanilla refilled caches (`Asteroid_inv*`, `nych`);
  - >= 600 from the points already kept in the system (`sep`).
- Caps: per region the candidates in the order (weight descending, never-named first (`TRF_StrictFree`), map idx, x, z), at most 2 per system and 8 per region; at most 60 rows (`--check` exits 1 above).
- Report: one line per region (rows, systems, weight, kinds) and one per dropped candidate with the tally of its failing rules (`b_leftera3 sr no point: slot 13, slot+ast 9`); the summary `survey: 46 landmarks in 8 regions, weight 106`.

Probe result (data note 2): 46 rows: Dosis 8, Elinor 8, Gefest 3, Golkonda 8, Kadi 8, Moscowia 5, Torr 4, XT-27 2 (kinds: `hk` 17, `cs` 11, `sr` 6, `rp` 5, `gr` 5, `wf` 2; 14 rows in never-named systems, 18 in dead ends). The generator's table is binding.

### 4.2 Open rows (`TRF_SyLmWhy(id, use)` -> nil or a reason; `use` "count" applies 1-3, "name" applies 2-4)

1. Survey closed and the row not a named row of an active quest 4 at step 1 with its mask bit clear -> `closed` (6.1).
2. Read already -> `read`.
3. `rp` row and `GetQuestLabel("OnRandomPortal_all")` (protected) not `"yes"` -> `rp_closed`.
4. `TRF_StrictOk(r.s, "survey")` fails -> `gate:<why>`; `TRF_N("TRF_Wn_Armed", r.s) == 1` -> `armed` (D11).

Survey open: cfg `sy_gate` 0, or `TRF_IqDone(4)`, or the debug cfg `survey_at` names the current system.

### 4.3 Card picks (`TRF_SyPickOf(ids, dist, h, why)`, pure)

For the dock's region slot: the region's row ids with `TRF_SyLmWhy(id, "name")` nil, ordered by the jump distance of the row's system from the dock system (`TRF_RgDistFrom`), then by `TRF_SyHashOf(legs, cur, id)`; the first three in distinct systems, then repeats when the region has fewer than three systems with an open row. Fewer than three rows -> no offer (`reason=few`). The ids go into the record (3.3) at the accept and are not recomputed.

| R6 | visit latch: `TRF_Safe_QState()` seen this visit (`SY.q`); at a dock also `TRF_PlQuest()` | all | `latch` / `mission` |
| R7 | hold: `__disableAction`, `__sys_SOS`, `TRF_Supp() ~= 0`, `TRF_Safe_Scene()` (scene first, SG deviation 6); at a dock `TRF_Quiet()` | all | `hold:<disable_action/sys_sos/scene/suppressed>` / `quiet` |
| R8 | `dt_game > 0`; the mothership position readable (`TRF_Ms()`, protected `GetPosition`) | tick | (no line) |
| R9 | the row open (4.2) | tick, near line, markers | (4.2 reasons, level 2) |
| R10 | `rp` row: distance to the portal >= `sy_rp_min` | tick | `rp_near` (level 2, once per visit) |

## 6. Flow

### 6.1 Entry and load (`TRF_Sy_OnActivate`, last module call of `TRF_OnActivate` after `TRF_Sg_OnActivate`; `TRF_Sy_OnLoad`, v1 load list)

1. Visit state fresh (MapInitialization); `TRF_Safe_QState()` -> `SY.q = 1`.
2. R1-R6 (cached); a skip ends here (the step retries every second, so a latch set at the entry holds for the visit).
3. `SY.lm` = the rows of `TRF_CurIdx()` with `TRF_SyLmWhy(id, "count")` nil.
4. Quest markers: for each named row of an active quest 4 at step 1 in this system with its mask bit clear: `TRF_MarkerAdd("survey", "TRF_SY_NP_"..id, x, y, z, "", K.icon, nil)` (text `#TRF_synav` with `loc_mode 1`, the stash V12 precedent); a refusal (`quest`, `cap`) retries at each 1 s step with `q` 1. trf_plumb deletes the markers before every jump and at a load, and refuses them during a mission.
5. Log `event=enter system= landmark_count= open=<quest/survey/closed> marker_count=` (level 2).

### 6.2 Near line and hold (`TRF_Sy_Step` every 1 s; `TRF_Sy_Tick(dt)` every tick after `TRF_Sg_Tick`)

Step: refresh `SY.ok`; rebuild `SY.lm` when the quest or the survey state changed; markers retry; for each row in `SY.lm` with `SY.nr[id]` nil and the mothership within `sy_near_r`: line `synear` (kind phrase, rounded distance, `sy_read_r`, `sy_hold`), `SY.nr[id] = 1`, `event=near`.
Tick (`dt` = `dt_game`):
1. `SY.lm` empty or `SY.ok` nil -> return. R7, R8 (a hold flag pauses: `h` kept, `event=hold result=paused` once per visit at level 2).
2. Position `b`; for each row: `d` = distance from `b` to the reading point.
3. `d > sy_read_r`, or an `rp` row with `d < sy_rp_min`: `h[id] = 0` (`event=hold result=reset` at level 2 when it was above 0).
4. Else `h[id] = h[id] + dt`; at the first tick of a hold `syhold` (once per row and visit, `event=hold result=start`); `h[id] >= sy_hold` -> the reading (6.3), `SY.lm` drops the row.

### 6.3 Reading (`TRF_SyRead(id, idx)`)

1. R1-R7 again; the bit set (SD1); `TRF.sy` written.
2. Fee: `F = floor(TRF_PayN(idx) x sy_fee_k / 100 x w x (1 + sy_thr_k / 100 x thr))` (`thr` 0..2 thresholds passed before this reading), paid as `TRF_Pay(F, "survey")`; 0 when `sy_fee_k` is 0 or the pay failed (text `syread0`).
3. Payoff, first that answers (`TRF_SyPayoffOf(id, have)`): P0 trail (`TRF_N("TRF_Tr_SurveyFrag", id)` -> text; absent today); P4 chart, when this reading completes the region (6.4); then, starting at `1 + mod(id, 3)` and going round, P1 signal, P2 base, P3 route:
   - P1 signal: `TRF_N("TRF_Sg_SlotRec", slot)` answers a record with `t` 0 (lit, unheard) in another system: `sypsig` ($1 the English system name). A08 then reads and marks it by its own rules.
   - P2 base: a `TRF.base` record (`TRF_Ba_Recs()`) with `k` 2 in a system of the slot other than the current one, the lowest map idx: `sypbase` ($1 system). Never a stash (D7).
   - P3 route: the nearest other row of the region that is open for both uses (`TRF_SyLmWhy` nil for "count" and "name", so a quest run points only at its own named rows and never at an armed system; jumps from the current system, then id): `syproute` ($1 region, $2 place phrase `syplace`, $3 the route clause: `syjhere`, `syj1` with the first portal's destination, `syjn` with the jump count and that destination; the path from `TRF_RgDistFrom` over `TRF_RgAdj`).
   - none: `payoff=none` (the line is `syread` alone).
4. Quest: a named row of quest 4 at step 1: mask bit set, `TRF_IqSet(4, 1, t, c)`; at 7 `TRF_IqSet(4, 2, t, 7)`; the clause `syqcnt` ($1 count) or `syqall`; `TRF_MarkerClear("survey")`, then the markers of the remaining named rows of this system again.
5. Threshold: points before and after (sum of the weights of the read rows); a crossing of `sy_thr1` or `sy_thr2` adds `sythr` ($1 points, $2 the new bonus in percent) and logs `event=threshold`.
6. Line: `syread` ($1 kind phrase, $2 system, $3 fee) .. " " .. payoff, then `TRF_Br()` and the quest or threshold clause; one `TRF_Say(text, sy_secs)`.
7. Log `event=read`.

### 6.4 Chart completion

At the reading that sets the last bit of the region's rows: block `b = format("%d%03d", TRF_Phase(slot), mod(legs, 1000))`; P4 `sypchart` ($1 region); `event=chart result=complete`. A region whose rows include an `rp` row cannot complete before the network opens (chapter 8).

### 6.5 Jump, load, wind-down, repair

- Jump (`TRF_Sy_BeforeJump`, v1 jump list): holds are visit state; trf_plumb deletes the markers. Log only (level 2).
- Load (`TRF_Sy_OnLoad`): as 6.1 steps 1-4 (no near line before the first step).
- Wind-down (`TRF_Sy_WindDown`, trf_gen's v1 list): `TRF_MarkerClear("survey")`.
- `TRF_Sy_Fix` (from `TRF_Repair` after `TRF_Sg_Fix`): F1 a non-string `TRF.sy` -> nil; F2 the bits: characters outside `0-9a-f` -> `0`, padded or cut to `ceil(N / 4)`, bits above `N` cleared; F3 exactly `TRF_RgMap().n` blocks (missing `""`, extra dropped), a block other than `""`, `"s"` or four digits with the first in 0..5 -> `""`; F4 a `""` block of a region whose rows are all read -> rebuilt with the phase and leg now; a four-digit block of a region with an unread row -> `""`; F5 encode, nil when empty; then the quest checks of 3.3. `event=fix field=<bits/block/quest> result=<dropped/reset/rebuilt>` when anything changed.

## 7. The quest "Chart the frontier"

### 7.1 Step 0: the offer (trade dock)

### 7.2 Step 10: three readings

6.3 step 4. A reading of a named row in a strict-denied system or during a mission does not happen (R5, R6), so nothing is counted there.

### 7.3 Step 20: the hand-in (any trade dock)

### 7.4 Done

### 7.5 Logbook (quest entry `trf_sy`)

## 8. Dock line, chart sale and the Survey log

### 8.1 Trade dock line (`TRF_DockLine("trade", 75, "TRF_Sy_TradeLine")`: after the charter 70, before the debrief 80)

Gates: R1, R2, R3, R5 (dock system), R6 (`TRF_PlQuest()` and the latch), R7 (`TRF_Quiet()`); a skip returns nothing. Then, in this order, every result a line in the returned list:

2.
