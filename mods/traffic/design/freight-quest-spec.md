## 3. Data model

### 3.1 Saved fields (repaired by `TRF_Wq_Fix`)

`TRF.wq` is nil until the first offer is answered; code reads it only through `TRF_WqRec()` (indexing a nil `TRF.wq` raises an error in Lua 4). It stays outside `TRF.co`, which `TRF_Fr_Fix` rebuilds from a fixed list.

| Field | Range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `v` | 1 | version |
| `s` | 0, 10, 20, ... 90 | step: 0 declined, 10 waiting for the mayday, 20 report owed, 30 find the wreck, 40 find the fence, 50 crew held, 60 crew aboard, 70 offer pending, 80 done (also the grandfather rule), 90 closed (failed; not reachable in this design, kept by the repair) |
| `k` | 0..7 | bits: 1 the 8.5 help was counted on the Kestrel's raid, 2 grandfathered, 4 the wreck pick included debris6 (logbook `wqd30d`) |
| `n` | 0..9 | voids of step 1 |
| `dc` | leg | decline leg (s 0 only) |
| `cd` | leg | leg of the last void (the re-roll waits `wq_cd` legs) |
| `sd` | 1..9999 | seed of the rolls |
| `lv` | 1 | the Kestrel is on-screen (s 10 only): a load or an entry without a jump hook voids the event |
| `r` | 1..3 | step 1 result: 1 saved, 2 lost, 3 limped in |
| `p` | 1 | the limped-in line is due at the next entry (with `r` 3) |
| `ql` | 10..90 | step of the logbook entry (a step change replaces it) |
| `ws` | system idx | wreck system (s 30 and 40 only): golkonda, callipso or debris6 (I11) |
| `m` | 1, 3 | hold mode (s 50 only): 1 the clan base, 3 the hulk guard (I19) |
| `cg` | 0..999 | generation of the tipped captain (s 50 only, mode 1; I20) |
| `pn` | 1..3 | pods ejected (s 50 and 60 only; I25) |
| `pk` | 0..`pn` | pods picked (with `pn` only; I27) |
| `wd` | leg | leg of done (s 80 only; the entry is removed 10 legs later, I32) |

Rules (`TRF_Wq_Fix`, in `TRF_Repair` right after `TRF_Fr_Fix`): a non-table `TRF.wq` or one whose `s` is not a multiple of 10 in 0..90 is dropped; every field in range or dropped (a void leg or a done leg in the future too); `dc` only at step 0, `lv` only at 10, `p` only with `r` 3, `ws` only at 30 and 40 and only a wreck system (kept as saved while the map is unknown); a missing `ws` at step 30 is picked again (I11, `event=fix ... result=picked reason=ws`); `m` (1 or 3) and `cg` (a number 0..999) only at 50, `pn` and `pk` only at 50 and 60 (`pk` only with `pn`, at most `pn`), `wd` only at 80; s 60 without survive's quest carry (`TRF_Sv_QCarry()` 0, read after `TRF_Sv_Fix`: `TRF.sv` or its carry dropped) falls back to s 50 with `pn` and `pk` gone, so the hold runs again (`event=crew pods= picked= result=reset reason=no_carry via=fix`, `event=step step_before=60 step_after=50 reason=no_carry`; the next reconcile gives `wqd50`); with survivors off (`nil`) s 60 stays (open question 18); a missing `sd` is made again; unknown keys go. The repair rebuilds the table at every entry and load (callers hold `TRF.wq`, never an older reference).

Survivor records (`TRF.sv`, trf_survive, `D/survivors-spec.md` 3.1): a quest record carries `q = 1` (`b[i].q`), and the carry gets `c.q = 1` once a quest pod is aboard; `TRF_Sv_Fix` keeps both and takes the quest records first, so the one-record-per-system rule and the cap of 4 drop an ordinary record before a quest record (the kept records stay in their saved order). Grandfather rule (U5): no record and a ship in `TRF.co` (repaired just before) gives `{s=80, k=2}` and no logbook entry. Field names are not reserved save keys (`T/host_stubs.lua` `HOST_Reserved`).

### 3.2 Visit state (chunk local `WQ`, never saved; rebuilt by every MapInitialization)

`WQ.v.ev = {st (due, live, end), g (generation), sys, id (trader id), rn (raid id), res}`, `WQ.v.dec` (declined in this visit), `WQ.v.hold = {m, moved}` (the mode picked at this entry), `WQ.v.gd = {fl, sh, dead, k, g, st (live, dead, void), n, pw, gn}` (the guard: flight, ship handles, deaths, kills, generation, state, ships asked, power, group name), `WQ.v.hk` (the hulk handle), `WQ.offer = {sys, g, var}` (the open box), `WQ.fence = {sys, g, price}` (the open fence box), `WQ.tc` (the clamped tune table). The spawn timer is a `TRF_Due` entry (handle table `H.due`, dropped with the generation); its callback `TRF_Wq_SpawnDue` is a `TRF_Cb` shim. ClearGlobals at every entry (`D/mod-research/exe-hook-survey.md`) therefore voids an on-screen event after a jump or a load, which is the intended exploit rule (08 section 6 rule 2).

### 3.3 Size

Host grammar (`HOST_SaveSize`). `TRF.wq` with every field at its widest (a bound: the repair never keeps `dc`, `lv`, `p`, `ws`, `m`, `cg`, `pn`, `pk` and `wd` together): 248 bytes in commit 1 (`wq-b`, checked <= 260), 270 with `ws` (`wq-d`, checked <= 280), 379 with the commit 3 fields (`wq-f`, checked <= 390). The survivor `q` flags add 21 bytes a record and 21 on the carry: `TRF.sv` at its bound is 2,769 bytes (`sv-g`, checked <= 2,800). Full-state `v1-n`: 94,376 bytes (94,267 after commit 2, 94,140 after commit 1, budget 100,000). One record, no list: the count cap is 1; the quest records (the navigator's, the crew of XB-13) count against `sv_max` 4 as any survivor record.

## 4. Steps

Every step has no deadline, runs its seeds and dock lines only where `TRF_StrictOk(idx, "wq")` passes (a new feature name matters only for a per-feature `sys_deny` entry `e.wq == 0`, `S/trf_safety.script` `TRF_StrictOkOf`; "wq" is added to the feature list in the comment of `TRF_StrictOk`), shows markers only through `TRF_MarkerAdd` (refused during a mission) and keeps its on-screen state in the chunk local `WQ`.

### 4.1 Step 0: the overdue transport (the offer)

- Where: a trade docking at a loop stop of an eligible region: chapters 5-6 the Gefest and Golkonda regions (`TRF_FrEarly`, by the region's home system), every loop region from chapter 7 (`TRF_FrLoop`, `TRF_FrStopOf`).
- Gate `TRF_WqGate(cur)` (reasons in this order): `off` (cfg `enable_wq` 0), `freight_off`, `nomap`, `chapter` (< 5), `chapter_end` (> 18), `mission`, `nk` (New Kyoto), `grandfather` (a ship in `TRF.co`), `started` (`s` other than 0), `declined` (`legs - dc < 10`, or a Cancel in this visit), `heat` (> 2), `war` (Trade at war), `help` (< `wq_help`, default 2), `no_loop`, `chapter_region`, `strict:<why>`.
- Presentation (U8, as trf_parcel): trade box `TRF_DockBox("trade", 450, "TRF_Wq_DockBox")` with the spec's `card` field (`id` "wq", header `wqjobh`, title `fwboxtitle`, no cost), so with cfg `jobs_ui` 1 and the native the box opens as the mission card (SJ 5.2); with `jobs_ui` 2 and the board natives the Office board row `TRF_JobRow("trade", 450, "TRF_Wq_JobRow")` (second line `wqjobs`) and no box (`TRF_JbSet`). 450 is free and above parcel 400, freight 300 and charter 200; one box per docking, so while the offer shows it hides the parcel offer and the freight purchase box at that dock (the board lists both rows).
- OK (`TRF_Wq_BoxOk`): the gate again (stale otherwise), the record at `s` 10 with its seed, the logbook entry (4.4), the clerk's reply. Cancel (`TRF_Wq_BoxNo`): `s` 0 and `dc` = the leg; no penalty.

## 11. How it fits

- Unlock rule: see 4.5 step 4 and I29. Help never decays below `help_floor` 10 but a failed parcel lowers it (`TRF_AddHelp(-d)`), so help 5 is nearly, not strictly, a one-time counter; the quest's hand-ins add about +3 help anyway. freight-spec G2 and its section 1 row "Unlock" name the quest gate.
- Existing saves: a ship in `TRF.co` gives s 80 (the grandfather rule, U5); a save with help >= 5 and no ship plays the quest (the offer at its next eligible dock).
- Charter goals: independent; no note on done (I33).
- Wanted captains: the tip removes one informant sale (`TRF_CapIntelPick` skips tipped records); with captains off, lying low or below tier 2 the step runs without him (the open-band line then speaks for the clan only when no captain lives).
- Haunts and stashes: xb13 is a pirate-flight and a stash system with four slots; the hulk and the pods stand 789 and 711 units from the nearest slot (I22); vanilla's `pir09` route at (948, 0, 608) and the clan's haunt flights can join the fight at the hulk.
- Bases: the hulk point is 200 units from the base site, so a D record's wreck at the site (cfg `wreck`) and the hulk do not overlap.
- Survivors: quest records count against `sv_max` 4 and the carry limit (`sv_carry_max` 6, two loss systems) as any; the full list drops ordinary records only (I17); a quest carry fills a carry slot until it is handed in, so it can refuse ordinary pods of a third loss system.
- trf_station's barge already uses "a trader plus a small raid" with `sev`; the Kestrel copies that path (the trader through trf_gen, the resolver, the 1 s step) rather than adding a third variant.
- Vanilla quests with the same fiction: Alex's mail_005 / mail_007 (the missing transports, which the mod continues and does not close; mail_007 is removed in mission_9b_p2); mission_4_52 Find_Matt (a pod rescue of a trader in Gefest and Elio, chapter 4); mission_5_1 and the LostMission scripts (the Quest5Freight sub-plot, excluded from the mayday systems); missions 6c, 6c_1, 6c_2 (FTU transport escorts in chapter 6, so the mayday repeats a beat); 7f and 8d (a wrecked InoCo convoy, Beta Lethera: the wq texts name neither).

Each page is `<message><Speaker>: #<token>#</message>`; answers are `- #<token>#` (the mod's dash, as the hail and merc pages); page links and [back] are `go_message_name` with `<lua_function />`; answers with an effect or that close the window call one shim `TRF_WqDlgA<k>_<kind>`; `multi_pass` true everywhere. The init `TRF_WqPgInit<k>` sets every token (`TRF_WqTxt(key, $1, $2, $3)`, a variant key where the variant differs) and shows or hides every answer (a reused Dialog object keeps its hidden flags).

Prefix rule: every `<message>` starts with the speaker's label as plain text (`FTU clerk: #p1#`, `Open band, #who#: #p1#`), because hail-dialog.md does not show that the window names the speaker (2.6 display: inferred) and a message starting with `#` is looked up as one key (3.6). `run_host.sh` fails on `<message>#`; `wq-g` checks the form `Speaker: #token#`.

Dock dialogs (1, 3, 3b, 5, 6, 9, 10) are boxes of the plumbing opened as a dialog: a box spec with `dlg` (a function name) makes `TRF_PlShow` call it with `(s.a, s)` after the box lock; 1 = the dialog opened (`s.shown(a, "dialog")`), 0 = no window and no box (`s.fail`: the talk box says its line and applies its effect), anything else = the card or the box as before. The owner answers with `TRF_PlDlgAnswer(a, esc)`: 1 or 0 as OK or Cancel (the handler, then the vanilla dock call once), -1 neither (the vanilla call once); `esc` 1 (Esc, seen from a due call of the tick) runs the handler without the vanilla call (as the Cancel of the vanilla dock request: the player docks again), so the vanilla dock code never runs from a tick. A final answer runs its effects in the answer callback, as a box button does. A load or jump drops an open dialog box (`TRF_PlCardDrop`). The dock lines of D3, D6 and D9 (trade line 40, patrol line 45, survive's hand-in line 30) leave their text to the talk box 460 of the same docking (`TRF_Wq_TalkBox`, above every other box) when the dialog gate is open; otherwise they say the line and apply the effect as before. A docking whose box path is blocked (another box holds the lock, the fee-box rule) shows no talk: D3 and D6 come at the next docking (the step did not move), D9 is lost (the letter P2 carries its content).

The Office board: the click on the Jobs row opens the DLL's mission card at once (`TRF_JobBoard`, pe/API.md), which Lua cannot replace; the card's OK opens D1 over the Office window (the gate allows the modal window flag 2, not a movie or another dialog), and its answers act directly (accept, decline). With the dialog unavailable the card's OK accepts as before.

In-space dialogs (2, 4, 7, 8) open from a due call (`TRF_WqSpace`, `TRF_Wq_SpaceDue`) under the box lock `wq` (freed at the end), one pending per visit (a second one says the first one's line). The gate `TRF_WqDlgWhy`: cfg `wq_dialog` 1, `hail_xml` 1, `StartTradeDialog`, no wq dialog open, `TRF_UiState()` 0 in space, without the movie and dialog flags (1, 4) at a dock (the dock request box's callback may leave the modal flag 2 up, as the DLL's mission card and vanilla's `OnCStationStart` allow) and on the Office board, `TRF_InMovie()` not 1, no story mission, and in space no scene flag (`__disableAction`, `__sys_SOS`, trf_safety suppression) and no jump in progress (a result saved at a jump says the thanks line: a due call would be dropped with the generation). A due check 1 s after the open (the clock stands while the window is up) ends a window that closed without a final answer as Esc.

### 13.2 Texts

127 keys in `TRF_Wq_Text` (39 before): the logbook `wqt`, `wqd10`..`wqd80` rewritten in the script's voice (English map names, no "clause", no "office remembers"); the fallback lines and boxes (`wqbox`, `wqboxo`, `wqok`, `wqoko`, `wqthx`, `wqthxo`, `wqlost`, `wqalone`, `wqrep1`-`3`, `wqnav`, `wqfence`, `wqfenceok`, `wqfencepoor`, `wqpatrol`, `wqband`, `wqclan`, `wqmaster`, `wqhome`, `wqmay`, `wqjobs`; freight's `fwboxq`) rewritten to one line each in the guide's voice; the pages `wq<n>p<x>` and answers `wq<n>a<x>` of D1-D10 exactly as the script; the letters `wqlt`, `wql1b`, `wql2b`. `$1`..`$3` are `TRF_WqTxt` arguments (the `$n` placeholders of `TRF_Txt`); none holds `%`, starts with `#` or exceeds 480 characters; `loc/trf_en.loc` gets them through `gen_map.py --loc` (RU: EN fallback). Names: the English map names through `TRF_WqSysEn(idx)` (CombineStr of the vanilla key `#M_location_<name>`, else the table `K.en`, else `TRF_UI_SysName`); `TRF_UI_SysName` itself returns the map folder name (`Gefest`), so it is the last fallback only. The clan is `TRF_ClanName(TRF_RgOf(xb13))` (Grey Ravens on the vanilla map), where the script says "Golconda clan" (D5's last page, D7's speaker and `wqband`).

Shipped keys outside the quest that still carry internal system names (not changed here): the news headlines and bulletins of regions 3, 4 and 8, `TRF_N_r3_*`, `TRF_N_r4_*`, `TRF_N_r8_*` (52 lines each, `gen_news.py` fills `$1` from `map.script`'s `dir`: `Gefest`, `Golkonda`, `xt27`); trf_charter's titles `chtgefest`, `chtgolkonda`, `chtxt27` ("FTU charter: Gefest region"); trf_parcel's destinations `pclq14`, `pclq22`, `pclq45` and `pclqd14`, `pclqd22`, `pclqd45` (`Callipso`, `Debris6`, `Golkonda`; generated from the folder names by `gen_parcel.py`). Lines built at run time through `TRF_UI_SysName` (the arrival lines, trf_freight's `fwbox*` with `$2` the region home, the station and haunt lines) show the same folder names.

### 13.7 Fallback

Every conversation keeps its earlier form, rewritten in the script's voice, when the gate is shut: cfg `wq_dialog` 0 (no log line), `hail_xml` not 1 (`xml`), no `StartTradeDialog` (`native`), no `TRF_UiState` (`uistate`), a movie, modal window or dialog up (`ui=<v>`), a movie (`movie`), a story mission (`mission`), the scene flags in space (`disable_action`, `sys_sos`, `suppressed`), a jump in progress (`jump`), a wq dialog open (`busy`), the box lock held in space (`lock`), the due list full (`due`: the Esc check cannot be armed), a failed call (`error`), a second in-space dialog (`replaced`): `module=wq event=dialog_fallback dialog=<k> reason=<why>`. D1, D5 and D10 fall back to their box (D1 also to the card); D3, D6 and D9 to their dock line with the effect at once; D2, D4 and D8 to the radio line; D7 to the arrival line. The fence box keeps its wallet gate in the fallback.
