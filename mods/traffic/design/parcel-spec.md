# Sealed parcels: implementation spec (proposal 1, step 1)

Status: step 1 implemented and host-tested 2026-10-24 (`tests/host_parcel.lua`, runs `pcl-a`..`pcl-h`), not run in the game. Design: `D/worldbuilding/01-sealed-parcels.md` (cited as "01 n.n"); shared rules: `D/worldbuilding/00-economy-and-progression.md` sections 6 and 8 ("00"); plumbing: `D/wb-plumbing-spec.md` ("WB"); shortages: `D/v02-spec.md` 7.2 and WB 11.
Paths: `D` = `mods/traffic/design`, `S` = `mods/traffic/Data/Scripts/Mods/Traffic`, `T` = `mods/traffic/tests`, `G` = the game's `Data`.
Module: new `S/trf_parcel.script`, prefix `Pc` (`TRF_Pc_*` public, `TRF_Pc<X>` internal), log lines `module=parcel` through `TRF_PcE` / `TRF_PcDE`. Static data: the chapter deny sets `TRF_PcDenyChData()`, generated by the new `tools/gen_parcel.py` from `G/Game/Quests.xml`.

## 3. Data model

### 3.1 Saved fields (repaired by `TRF_Pc_Fix`; version stays 4)

`TRF.job` (the plumbing job slot, WB 3; at most 6 fields besides `k`): `k` 1 plain, 2 relief (3 contraband and 4 prototype are step 2; 5 is #5's favour and is never touched here), and the packed fields (`TRF_PcPackOf` / `TRF_PcUnpackOf`):

| Field | Packing | Design fields |
|---|---|---|
| `r` | `src x 1000 + dst` | `src`, `dst` (1..113) |
| `t0` | accept leg | `t0` |
| `ml` | 0..999 | `ml` (legs spent in missions) |
| `pd` | `pay x 100000 + dep` | `pay` 0..30000, `dep` 0..60000 |
| `m` | `it + 10 n + 100 j + 1000 dl + 100000 cls + 1000000 sj + 10000000 fac + 100000000 ssj + 1000000000 wv` | `it` 1..4, `n` 1..3, `j` 1..6, `dl` 5..20 (`3J + 2`), `cls` 0..6, `sj` the destination's trade station (1..2, replaces `dp`), `fac` 0..5, `ssj` the source's trade station (1..2; 0 in a job packed without it: the failure deed then picks the station by position), `wv` 1 when the deposit was waived (the failure penalty reads it, not `dep = 0`) |
| `c` | `cu x 100 + cs x 10 + hot` | `cu`, `cs`, `hot` (step 2, 0) |

`TRF.pcl` (created at the first accept): `ok`, `fail` (0..999 lifetime counters, #14 reads them), `je` (jump stamp of the last job's end; `jn` of the design is `TRF_Jumps() - je`), `ban` (offers refused until this leg), `lsys`, `lleg`, `lok`, `lit` (last finished job: destination, leg, 1 delivered, item for the thank-you wording), `lt` (1: its thank-you line was shown), `pm`, `pr` (pending line at the next trade dock: 1 late, 2 void, 3 empty, 4 void without units; the refund), `ow` (pay held by the prize gap, 1..999,999), `pq` (1: the logbook entry is done or failed and is removed at the next trade dock), `ll` (last leg the mission-leg counter saw); step 2: `xl[rg]`, `cfl[f]`.

`TRF.short[i].job` (00 section 6): 1 while a relief parcel targets the entry (`TRF_ShortJob` in trf_shop); `TRF_ShortRelief` already skipped `pk = 1` and `job = 1` (WB 11).

Rules (`TRF_Pc_Fix`, from `TRF_Repair` at every entry and load, after `TRF_Pl_Fix`): a non-table `TRF.pcl` is dropped; every field within its range (a wrong value is dropped; `ok`, `fail` read 0; `pm` without `lsys` dropped; a past `ban` dropped; `xl` keeps number pairs of slots 1..16, `cfl` a list of 5 numbers); a `TRF.job` of kind 1..4 that does not unpack (`kind`, `field`, `system`, `range`, `class`) is dropped (`event=repair`); a shortage `job` flag without the matching open relief job is cleared.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `v` | visit flags: `dec` (an offer declined here), `cold` (the cold line shown), `probe` (the inventory probe ran) |
| `offer` | the offer of the open box (5.4) |
| `adj`, `lw`, `dch` | caches: the portal adjacency (`TRF_RgAdj`), `L_w` per system, the chapter deny sets |
| `tc` | the clamped tune table and the `TRF_Tune` table it was built from |

Marker label `TRF_PCL_NP`, owner `parcel`; logbook id `trf_pcl`.

### 3.3 Size

Host grammar (`HOST_SaveSize`: number 12, string 8 + n, key 8 + n). Worst case measured in `pcl-h` and `v1-n`: `TRF.pcl` with every field at its widest and the step 2 fields (`xl` in 11 slots, `cfl`) 769 bytes with its key (checked <= 800); the packed job at its widest 173 bytes (inside the plumbing's 6-field worst case of 296, WB 12); the `job` flag on 8 shortage entries 8 x 23 = 184. Typical open job: about 170 + 200 bytes. Full-state `v1-n` with everything on: 89,298 bytes (budget 100,000; 88,323 before). The design's "under 1 KB" holds.

## 5. Mechanics

### 5.1 Offer gates (`TRF_PcGate(cur, sj)`, 01 sections 3, 4)

In this order, the first that fails is the debug reason of `event=offer ... result=none`: cfg `enable_parcels` 1 (`off`); chapter >= 4 (`chapter`, `TRF_Chapter`); free roam: `GetPlayerQuestState() ~= TRUE` or `quest_Help` in process (`mission`); `TRF_Quiet` (`quiet`); the strict gate `TRF_StrictOk(cur, "parcel")` (`strict:<why>`); the job slot free (`job`); not banned (`ban`, with the cold line); `pcl_gap` jumps since the last job ended (`gap`); heat <= 2 (`heat`); not declined in this visit (`declined`); a trade station in the map entry (`station`); under the guard (chapter <= 4) the system is Aurora, Corrino or Pegas (`guard`); the source passes the end rules of 5.2 (`deny:<why>`); outside the guard `pro >= 3` (`pro`); #3 standing `st >= 0` at the docked station (`dockmaster`).

### 5.2 Exclusions and static data

- Route exclusion (`TRF_PcRouteEx`): the owner at war with the player (`GetTeamRelation(GetLocationTeam(name), PLAYER_TEAM) == RELATION_WAR`, `hostile`); the InoCo core (Ashelon, Danstag, Hafa, xk10) unless `InocoPermit` is `"yes"` or InoCo is FRIEND (`inoco`).
- End rules for source and destination (`TRF_PcEndEx`): the static deny list (Fronn, nk, Earth, Precursors, DisonSphere, x203, x213, x220, x297; `static`), cfg `pcl_deny` (`cfg`), the chapter deny set of the current chapter (`chapter`), a deny entry with `parcel = 0` (`deny_entry`), then the route rules.
- Chapter deny sets: `tools/gen_parcel.py` writes `TRF_PcDenyChData()` (the `<location>` systems of every `mission_<c>*` of `G/Game/Quests.xml`, lowercase, by chapter; checks: every name a map system, chapter 4 = Ahilles, Argon, Elio, Gefest, Gredos, chapter 8 the largest with 31, chapter 20 only nk); cfg `pcl_deny_ch` `{[c] = {names}}` adds names.
- Trade docks: the map's `st` entries of kind `trade`; the dock param is the station's `prm`, else the location folder (Kadenoa: `kadenoa` and `kadenoa_tri`).

### 5.3 Offer plan (`TRF_PcPlan(cur, sj)`, 01 sections 5, 6)

- Candidates: trade-dock systems at 2..`pcl_jmax` (4) jumps (1..`pcl_jmax_g` (3) under the guard, and only Aurora, Corrino, Pegas) by BFS over the portal adjacency without route-excluded systems (checked for the systems within `pcl_jmax + 1` jumps only), passing the end rules, not the source.
- Relief: among `TRF_ShortRelief()` entries (no `pk`, no `job`) on a candidate, the fewest jumps, then the lower idx, then the lower class. The offer exists when `(src x 7 + floor(legs / rg_tick) x 13) mod 100 < pcl_offer_rpct` (80) with a relief target, else `< pcl_offer_pct` (60) (`roll`). Without a relief target a plain parcel needs a second fixed hash `(src x 3 + floor(legs / rg_tick) x 29 + 41) mod 100 < pcl_plain_pct` (50) (`plain_roll`, V4) and goes to the candidate with the lowest `pro`, then fewer jumps, then the lower idx.
- `J` = BFS jumps; `L_job` = `TRF_PcLJobOf`: the minimum over walks of at most `J + 1` jumps that avoid route-excluded systems of the highest `L_w` on the walk (source excluded); `T_job` = the highest `TRF_EffTier` on the BFS route (source excluded), T1 under the guard.
- Pay `min(r100(pcl_k x J x 900 x L_job x lk x tier_k(T_job) x (1 + 0.05 pro(src)) x d_k x s_k), pay_cap)` with `lk = min(1, L_job / 8)`, `s_k` = `pcl_relief_k` (1.25) for relief, 1 plain.
- Item: `Cargo_002` under the guard, for plain parcels and for classes 5-6; `Cargo_crate` for classes 1-4. Units `n = min(J, 3)` (V6). Deposit `floor(k x cost x n + 0.5)` with the map cost (`Cargo_crate` 1,200, `Cargo_002` 850) and `k` = `pcl_dep_k` (1.2) or `pcl_dep_k2` (0.5) for `Cargo_002`; 0 for `Cargo_002` at help >= `pcl_waive_help`. Deadline `dl = 3J + 2`. Destination station `sj`: 1, or by `(src + window) mod count` in a two-dock system.

### 5.4 Box and acceptance (`TRF_Pc_DockBox`, `TRF_Pc_BoxOk`, `TRF_Pc_BoxNo`)

- Box (title `pcltitle`): the shortage clause `pclneed<cls>` (relief), `pclbox` (units, destination, `pcljumps`), `pcldep` (deposit, pay) or `pclnodep` (waived). The plan is kept in `PC.offer`; the spec's `shown` handler writes `event=offer ... result=shown`.
- OK: the gates again with the same generation and system (`stale`), the credits for the deposit (`pclpoor`), `SubPlayerCredits`, `TRF_JobTake(k, packed)`, the units onto the mothership (`AddModuleToInventory`; on failure the deposit back and `pclfail`), the shortage's `job` flag, `TRF.pcl.ll`, the logbook entry; reply `pclhand`.
- Cancel: `PC.v.dec`, no offer again in this visit.

### 5.5 Travel and arrival

- Mission legs (`TRF_PcMlStep`, 1 s step, entry and dock): legs added since `TRF.pcl.ll` while `GetPlayerQuestState() == TRUE` add to `ml`; the entry leg of a jump during a mission counts.
- Arrival (`TRF_Pc_OnActivate`): the expiry and void checks (5.7); in the destination `TRF_ArrivalLine("parcel", pclmark)` and the marker (`TRF_MarkerAdd("parcel", "TRF_PCL_NP", station x, y, z, "", "nav_target.dds", station object)`; none during a mission, none when the strict gate denies the destination; trf_plumb deletes it before every jump and on load). Load in the destination: the marker again.

### 5.6 Delivery (trade line 20, `TRF_PcDeliver`)

At a trade dock of the destination whose param equals the job's dock: the counting delete loop (`while k < n and IsExist(item) do DeleteModule(item)`); `k = 0` is a failure (`empty`, 5.7); else `pay x k / n` and `dep x k / n`: the deposit part back (`AddPlayerCredits`), the pay through `TRF_Pay(.., "parcel")` with `TRF_PrizeMark`, or held in `TRF.pcl.ow` while `TRF_PrizeWait() > 0` (cfg `pcl_prize_gap` 1; the owed payer pays it later, `pclowe`; a payout short of the pay is owed the same way); help +1 (+2 when the destination's region is in Recovery); `TRF_ProCredit(2 plain / 4 relief)` (doubled in Recovery there); `TRF_Stn_Deed(dst, 1)`; a relief entry still active ends at the next cleanup (`TRF_ShortEnd`, line `pclshort`; the station paid the shortage price at line 10 of this docking). Lines `pcldone`, `pcldonew` (waived), `pcldoneh` (held), `pclpart` (k < n), `pclparth` (k < n, the pay part held). Then the end (5.8). Delivery runs during a mission too (no spawn; the strict gate is not asked).

### 5.7 Expiry, failure, void (`TRF_PcCheck` at arrival and at each trade dock, expiry first)

- Expiry: `legs - t0 - ml > dl` -> failure `late`.
- Failure (`TRF_PcFail`): the units found removed (V10), help -1 (-3 with the waived deposit, `wv`), the deposit kept, `ban = legs + pcl_ban`, `TRF_Stn_Deed(src, -1)` on the source station `ssj` (its position passed, so the deed does not depend on where the mothership is), `fail + 1`, the relief flag cleared; line `pcllate` (or `pclempty`) at the next trade dock (`pm`). During the ban every trade dock shows `pclcold` once per visit and offers nothing.
- Void (`TRF_PcVoid`): the destination now fails the end rules (static, cfg or chapter deny, owner at war, InoCo shut), or at arrival its station is found by label and not alive: the units found removed, `dep x k / n` refunded at the next trade dock (`pclvoid`; `pclvoid0` when there is nothing to refund), no penalty.

### 5.8 End and logbook

Every end (`TRF_PcEnd`): `je = TRF_Jumps()`, `lsys`, `lleg`, `lok`, `lit`; the logbook entry `QUEST_DONE` or `QUEST_FAILED` with `pq = 1`, removed at the next trade dock; `TRF_MarkerClear("parcel")`; `TRF_JobClear`. The thank-you line (`pclthx1` crates, `pclthx2` containers) shows once at the first docking in the destination within 10 legs of a delivery, in a later visit (`TRF_Jumps() > je`, so a load in the delivery visit does not count). Logbook (`trf_pcl`, `AddQuest(id, "#TRF_pclq", "#TRF_pclqd", QUEST_PROCESS)`; cfg `pcl_logbook` 0 drops it): reconcile at entry and load outside a mission: an open job whose entry reads `QUEST_NOINIT` is added again; an entry without a job and without a pending removal is removed.

### 5.9 Toggles off

With `enable_parcels` 0 nothing runs (no box, no line, no check, no step); an open job waits with its items, and the checks run again when the module is on.

## 7. Log lines (`TRF_PcE`, `TRF_PcDE`; rows in `D/log-events.md`, section parcel)

`event=offer` (`shown`, `none reason=<gate or plan reason>`, `declined`, `failed`), `event=accept` (`ok`, `poor`, `stale`, `error`), `event=quest`, `event=reconcile`, `event=arrive`, `event=load`, `event=mission_legs`, `event=deliver`, `event=fail`, `event=void`, `event=pending`, `event=owe`, `event=thanks`, `event=dock`, `event=signal`, `event=probe`, `event=repair`, `event=dump`; trf_shop `event=shortage ... result=job` / `result=relieved`; trf_plumb `event=job`, `event=arrival_line owner=parcel`, `event=marker owner=parcel`, `event=prize owner=parcel`, `event=dock kind=trade ... box=parcel priority=400 result=box`.

Mapping to the design's experiment lines (01 section 8): `TRF PCL E1 add=3 del=3 left=0` is `event=probe probe_kind=inventory added=3 deleted=3 left=false` (and `event=deliver ... units_found=<k>`); `TRF PCL E2 ... dcred=<n>` is two `event=probe probe_kind=credits credits=<n>` lines around a sale; `TRF PCL E3 box=1 ok=<0|1> dlg=1` is the plumbing's `event=dock ... box=parcel ... result=box`, then `event=accept ... result=ok` or `event=offer ... result=declined`, then `event=box_answer`; `TRF PCL E4 q=.. pqs_before=.. pqs_after=..` is `event=quest id=trf_pcl state=2 quest_state_before=<n> quest_state_after=<n> result=added`; `TRF PCL E5 sys=.. param=.. cur=..` is `event=dock system=<s> param=<p> dock_current=<n>`; `TRF PCL E6 pqs=.. help=.. cpq=.. cq=..` is `event=signal at=<enter, dock> quest_state=.. quest_help=.. count_pause_quest=.. current_quest=..`.

## 11. Deviations

- V1 Job record: the design's 17 named fields do not fit the plumbing slot (at most 6 fields, WB 3); they are packed into `r`, `t0`, `ml`, `pd`, `m`, `c` (3.1). `dp` is not stored: the station index `sj` is, and the dock param is derived from the map (`prm`, else the folder).
- V2 `jn` is the stamp `TRF.pcl.je` against the shared `TRF.clock.j` (00 section 6, one jump counter); the dump shows `jumps_since`.
- V3 `L_job` is computed at run time per offer (minimax over the portal adjacency, `TRF_PcLJobOf`) instead of an offline table per pair (M13); `T_job` follows the BFS route. The chapter deny sets are generated offline as designed (`tools/gen_parcel.py`).
- V4 The plain parcel share `pcl_plain_pct` uses a second fixed hash of the same window; the design gives the chance, not the draw.
- V5 Guard docks: on the map Corrino-Pegas is 4 jumps (the design says 3 from both); with J 1..3 under the guard that pair is never offered. Aurora-Corrino 1 and Aurora-Pegas 3 are as designed.
- V6 Units: `n = min(J, 3)` (the design gives 1..3 and the example 3 crates at J 3).
- V7 Plain parcels carry `Cargo_002` (general freight); under the guard every parcel does.
- V8 `lk` (00 A3, decided) is in the pay, so early pay is below the design's early figures.
- V9 The shared prize gap (B1) applies to parcel pay because the brief asks for it (cfg `pcl_prize_gap` 1, as Wolves Freight); the held pay is an owed payment, the deposit refund is never held.
- V10 A failure removes the units found (the design leaves them in the hold); the deposit is kept, so unsellable `Cargo_002` does not stay as junk and a sold crate costs the same.
- V11 A delivery with no unit aboard is a failure (`empty`), not a zero-pay delivery.
- V12 The parcel lines show at trade docks only; the failure line "on the next docking anywhere" shows at the next trade docking.
- V13 The thank-you line stays a parcel dock line (trade line 20); #3 has no provider hook for its trade-dock greeting, so it is not moved there.
- V14 The logbook description is generic ("the trade station marked on your map"): one fixed key, no key per destination.
- V15 A dead destination station is one the label lookup returns and `IsAlive` denies; a label the engine does not answer is not taken as dead.
- V16 Mission legs are counted in the 1 s step (and at entry and dock) against the saved last leg `TRF.pcl.ll`, not in `TRF_OnTick`.
- V17 The box text is built from several keys (`TRF_Txt` takes three arguments); with `loc_mode` 1 it shows several keys.
- V18 Extra offer gates: `TRF_Quiet` and the strict gate (feature `parcel`) at the source (01 "otherwise the traffic strict gate decides").
- V19 A relief delivery ends the shortage by setting its `till` to the current leg (`TRF_ShortEnd`): it is inactive at once and dropped at the next cleanup; this docking's shortage price was written at line 10 before.
- V20 Kadenoa as a destination: the dock is chosen by the window hash; the box names the system only.
