# Star Wolves 3: Civil War: existing systems a traffic mod builds on

Scope: what the shipped game (GOG v1.12) already has for traders, trade, factions, portals, escorts, news and persistence. Paths are relative to `Data/` under the game dir (`/Users/secustor/Games/Heroic/Star Wolves 3 - Civil War/Data`). Scripts are Lua 4.0, cp1251. Engine-side facts come from `strings` on `sw3cw.exe` and are marked "(exe)". Anything inferred and not verified at runtime is marked "(inferred)".

## 0. Script lifecycle (needed to read the rest)

- `Scripts/MapInitialization.script` loads `Scripts/system.lst` (43 include files, all of `include/*` and `AI/*`). Each include ends with its own `__initialize_*()` call, so module state (contact tables, timers) is reset every time the system scripts load.
- Each of the 114 systems has `Scripts/Locations/<Name>/location.script` (environment, portals, stations, nav points; MapBuilder2 output), `functions.script` (rebinds objects with `GetObjectByLabel`, portal conditions and actions, `TeleporterAction`) and `activate.script` (teams and groups, flights, docking triggers, random contacts, mini-quests). The exe references all three names (exe).
- `activate.script` creates every NPC flight again on each entry (for example `Locations/Gredos/activate.script` spawns 3 traders, 12 VKS flights and 7 pirate flights). NPCs do not persist between visits (inferred: flights are created without any "already exists" check).
- Story missions attach per-location scripts through `Game/Quests.xml` (193 missions, 308 `<action>` entries, each `<action name=X><location>Y</location>`). An action script defines `ACTION_INIT`, `ACTION_LEAVELOCATION` and `ACTION_FINI` (example `Scripts/Quests/mission_6c_1/Convoy1_NJ2.script`).
- Only one location is loaded and simulated at a time. Nothing in the scripts runs for a system the player is not in.

## 1. Random contacts and flying traders

File: `Scripts/include/RandomContacts.script`; flight builders in `Scripts/include/Create_flight_2.script`.

Registration and generator
- `RegisterRandomContact(contacttype, groupName, portalList, navPointList, probability, level)` appends to `__sys_LevelRegisteredContacts`. `contacttype` is the name of a builder function (`TRADER_FLIGHT = "R_CreateTraderFlight"`, `PIRATE_FLIGHT`, `VKS_FLIGHT`, `KFNI_FLIGHT`, `USS_FLIGHT`, `INOCO_FLIGHT`, `TRIADA_FLIGHT`, `PATROL_FLIGHT`, `HUNTER_FLIGHT`, `BERSERK_FLIGHT`).
- `StartRandomContacts(firstInterval, interval)` creates one `_EVENT_TIMER` trigger. `trigger_generator_Action` re-arms itself every `interval` seconds, picks one registered contact uniformly (`GetRandomContact`), rolls its probability (`ComputeProbability`) and spawns at most one flight per tick. `DisableRandom()/EnableRandom()` set `__disableAction`.
- Usage across `Locations/*/activate.script`: 96 systems call `StartRandomContacts`. Common settings are `(100,120)` in 26 systems and `(80,120)` in 22; the rest range from `(60,100)` to `(100,180)`. 80 systems register a trader contact (83 active `RegisterRandomContact(TRADER_FLIGHT,...)` lines; 22 more are commented out). Trader probabilities are mostly 30 % (31 systems) or 40 % (20 systems), range 5 to 70 %. Faction warship contacts are fewer (VKS 19, PIRATE 19, USS 12, TRIADA 12, INOCO 12, KFNI 11).
- Example, Gredos: one contact (trader, 60 %, level 8), interval 120 s, so a trader appears about every 200 s of game time.

What a spawned contact does
- Strength = `level` plus a random offset of -4 to +3, clamped to 1..20. `NewAllFlight` maps strength through a per-difficulty table to a row of the composition table.
- Trader composition `Trad_merc_Ships` (20 rows): a transport (`HMQueen_tr*`, `Walrus_tr*`, `Btransport`, `Btransport2`, `Arba_tr*`) plus 1 to 4 mercenary fighters, formation `shipFormationConvoy`. Pilots come from `Trad_merc_Pilots` ("Trader", "GreenRecruit*").
- Cargo: `FillCargoFlight(flight, Trad_CargoType, power, 100, 6)` puts 1 to 3 + 1 to 6 items from `Trad_CargoType` (containers `Cargo_01..10`, `Barrel_02`, `Uranium`) into each big ship's inventory. The cargo is random and has no origin or destination.
- Path: start at a random portal from the contact's list (`portal:Start(8,0)` plays the portal effect), fly `Patrol(path, FALSE)` (no loop) through the registered nav points, chosen at random with repetition, then to a different portal. When `GetRandomPortalIndexes` gets a list with one portal, the start and end portal are the same.
- Despawn: `trigger_exit_point` (on leaving the entry point) arms `trigger_inside2portal` (`_EVENT_FLIGHTINSIDEOBJECTVOLUME`, radius 20 around the end portal). That trigger starts the portal effect and calls `flight:Delete()`. Lifetime equals the time to cross the system. The trader leaves the simulation and never arrives anywhere.
- No NPC ever moves between systems. "Leaving through a portal" means deletion.

Meeting the player (only for `TRADER_FLIGHT` contacts and `MakeTrade(flight)`)
- `trigger_trader_meat`: when the flight is within 70 units of the player mothership, `_FTrader_Action` runs once. The trigger is never re-armed, so there is one interaction per trader flight. Conditions: the leader ship is a big ship, the trader group is not at `RELATION_WAR` with the player, `__sys_SOS == FALSE` (scripted scenes suppress traders through `_FTrader_exclude/_FTrader_include`), and `RAND(100) < chance1`.
- `_Trader_Init(chance1, chance2, chance3, chance4)` is called per system (24 systems use `(10,30,3,100)`, others `(60,5,3,100)`, `(70,5,3,100)`, `(20,30,3,0)` and so on). chance2 is multiplied by 3.5 and chance3 by 5 inside the function.
- Outcomes in order: (a) chance2: `ShowMsgBox("#UI_FTrader_confirm")`; on OK, `On_FTrader_Start` opens `TradeStationDlg` on a random shop from `ShopList_Trader` (`trader1..trader12`). (b) chance3: `_Trader_Speak` starts one of 17 `Scripts/Trade/DialogTrader{N}.xml` (or the `...n` variant if the player has under 5000 cr). Paying 5000 cr (`DialogTrader1_fin2`) adds logbook entry `book_traderN` and records it in quest label `Trader_Info` as a 17-character "0/1" string. (c) otherwise the pilot says one of 11 lines `#L_FT_Replica_01..11`.

When attacked
- Every trader flight gets `NewTrigger("attack", ..., Call_SOS_command, PLAYER_GROUP, ship)` (`_EVENT_ATTACKCARCASS`). The trader speaker says `#L_SOS_Replica_00`. After 8 s, `Call_SOS_command2` rolls `chance4` (`__sys_SOS_3`, usually 100) and spawns 4 flights of the system owner's faction (`GetLocationTeam(GetCurrentLocation())`, Neutral becomes Patrol). The flights appear at strength 18 to 20 around the mothership with portal-glow effects and attack it. The response does not scale with game progress.
- The engine also has `distanceToReportAggressiveBehaviour = 200` in `AI/AI.ini` (an attacked ship asks friends within 200 for help) (exe/ini).
- Relation change: the group relation `Trade_Group_1` to `PLAYER_GROUP` changes inside the engine. On exit through a standard portal, `TeleporterAction` writes group relations back into team relations with `SetInitTeamRelation`. Only 17 of the 80 trader systems write back "Trade" (`grep 'SetInitTeamRelation("Trade"' Locations/*/functions.script`), so attacking traders persists in some systems and is forgotten in others.
- Bounty and experience: `CostOfHead(pilot_name, count)` in `Scripts/include/CostOfPirates.script` is an engine callback (exe), called when captured pilots are handed over at a patrol station (`__PatrolStationDock_Ok` calls `InitExpForSaved/ExpForSaved`). Traders ("Trader", "TraderGuard", "UFTTrader") give 0.2 experience each and no credits. Pirates, Grey, Others and Templar pay `index^2 * 200` cr. Faction pilots pay unless they belong to the current system's owner.
- Loot: destroyed ships carry inventory filled by `FillCargoFlight`. `AI.ini [KeepLevel] moduleAdsorbtionRadius = 120` pulls "ownerless" modules into the mothership. Containers then sell at shops (see section 8) (inferred: drop on death is engine behaviour; not verified).

Other trader flavours (all in `Create_flight_2.script`)
- `NewTrader_SOS_Flight`: a cargo-only transport placed by `activate.script` on a looping `Patrol` between stations. 32 calls in 13 systems. These traders loop for the whole visit and have no trade dialog.
- `NewTrader_Trade_Flight` = `R_CreateTraderFlight` + `MakeTrade`. 10 calls in 8 systems.
- `SpecTraderFlight(..., faction)`: transports parked next to stations, inventory filled by `FillTraderSimple(ship, faction)` with faction-specific guns, missiles, systems, 1 to 2 fighters and 5 to 15 containers (`Scripts/include/FloodTradeStations.script`). 18 calls in 7 systems (for example Aurora, Dionis, El_ariash).
- `NewTraderFlight` (`Trad_BigShips`, no escort) is defined but has no callers in the location scripts.

Related ambient systems in the same pattern
- `Scripts/include/OutOfMission.script`: `OutMissionActivity(func, delay, prob)` is called from 113 systems' `activate.script`. It runs the mission-pause counter (`CountPauseQuest`/`PauseQuest` quest labels) and optionally schedules one mini-event: `RandomPiratGetMoney` (pirate extortion; 67 systems; only if Pirates are at war with the player and no quest is active; the demand scales with `GetPlayerLevel()` from the mothership hull) or `_PatrolGIBDD` (patrol fines the player 10 % of credits when the mothership stays within 40 units for 45 s; 25 systems).
- `OutMission_kill_pirate(flight1, flight2, cost, ...)`: a bounty mini-quest offered by one flight against another.
- `Scripts/include/RandomFmerc.script`: `RegisterNaemnik` (hireable mercenary clans). No current location script calls it.
- `Scripts/include/DefStation.script`: `DefStation(station, group)` puts a station in a unique "Defenders_" group. `Attack_Station_All` sets that group to war with the player and spawns defenders when the player attacks the station.
- `Scripts/include/Respawn.script` and `RespawnProg.script`: timer-based (15 s) respawn of defender flights around an anchor object with escort, attack or defence tasks. `RespawnProg` adds one more flight after each death.

## 2. Station shops

Files: `Scripts/InitTradeSystem.script`, `Scripts/include/FloodTradeStations.script`, `Scripts/include/DockStation.script`, `AI/SellingCoef.ini`.

- Shops are named objects created by `CreateShop(name)`: one per system (lowercase system name) plus `trader1..12` (flying traders), `pirate1..7` (contraband), `vulcan`, `simeiz`, `zebos`, `haron`. `CreateShopList()` creates them; quests add more (`CreateShop("mis15b")` in `Quests/mission_15b/IDots_XT05.script`). Each shop has a white market and a black market: `GetWhiteMarket(name)`, `GetBlackMarket(name)`.
- Shop lists by owner: `ShopList_NI` (17), `ShopList_SI` (29), `ShopList_Triada` (17), `ShopList_USS` (12), `ShopList_InoCo` (12), `ShopList_Neutral` (16), `ShopList_Rare` (4), `ShopList_Trader` (12), `ShopList_Kontraband` (7).
- Stock: `__InitTradeSystem()` runs once at new game. The exe references `Data\Scripts\InitTradeSystem.script` directly; it is not in `system.lst`. Per shop list it calls `AddAllToShops(list, NShop_X, Chance, Bye, Sell)` and `AddAllToBlackShops(...)`. For every item index `j` with max `n > 0`, `RAND(100) < Chance` decides whether the item is stocked and `RAND(n)+1` sets the count. `NShop_X` is a 4-part table (guns, systems, rockets, ships) aligned to `__All_guns`, `__All_systems`, `__All_rockets` and `__All_ships`. The function then sets quest label `__ShopCorrect = "true"`.
- Price: per item `ShipGoodsState(name, count, sell, buy)` and `ModuleGoodsState(name, count, sell, buy)`, where sell and buy multiply the base `<cost>` from `Game/Modules.xml` or the ship price. The coefficients depend on shop list and difficulty. White markets on normal difficulty use sell 1.35 and buy 0.6; neutral shops 1.75 and 0.4; black markets about 1.05 and 0.5; Zebos 0 and 0 (free). Other market methods (exe): `DefaultGoodsPrice(sell,buy)` and `CommonGoodsPrice(sell,buy)` (used once in `Quests/mission_16a_3/EmpireUnderFire_Simeiz.script` to make a shop free), `ClearGoodsState`, `ClearMarket`, `DestroyShop` (the last three are unused).
- `AI/SellingCoef.ini` has global multipliers `[Market]`, `[BlackMarket]` and `[Mothership]`, each with `buy=1` and `sell=1`.
- Changes over time: no script restocks shops or changes prices on a schedule. Changes happen only in quest scripts (for example `Quests/mission_10/FrontierSystems_X297.script` adds Stormcrow mk2 and Cleaner mk2 to `x297` and calls `UpdateTradeSystem(TRUE)`, 10 quest files in total). `CorrectOldShop()` in `FloodTradeStations.script` patches old saves once, on the first dock where `__ShopCorrect ~= "true"`. `UpdateTradeSystem(FALSE)` is called right before every `TradeStationDlg`; `TRUE` is used after a script edits stock. The exact meaning of the flag is not verified.
- Supply and demand: none in the scripts. Whether items the player sells are added to that shop's stock is engine behaviour and not verified. Stock state is saved (`STREAMID_TradeSystem`, exe).
- The 12 `trader*` shops are shared by every flying trader in the galaxy and are never refilled. Buying out `trader3` empties it for every later trader that rolls `trader3`.
- Docking: `CreateTradeStationDocking(station, mothership)` (76 calls) → `CreateDocking` makes inside, outside and 5 s wait triggers → `__Wait_Action` → `__TradeStation_Action` → msgbox → `__TradeStationDock_Ok` → `UpdateTradeSystem(FALSE); TradeStationDlg(GetCurrentLocation())`. Other station types use the same docking framework: `CreatePirateStationDocking` (11 calls, random or fixed `pirate*` shop, sets `SetTraderFlag(TRUE)`), `CreatePatrolStationDocking` (43; pilot hand-in through `PatrolStationDlg`), `CreateRepairStationDocking` (32; repair and AI chips through `StartTradeDialog("DialogR*")`), `CreateRelationStationDocking` (8; reputation reset), `CreateNoneStationDocking` (132; closed-station messages, including "trade complex closed due to blockade" (`"2"`) and "closed due to martial law" (`"3"`, `"3_vks"`)), `CreateBeacon`. `ChangeFuncDocking`, `StopDocking` and `StartDocking` can switch or disable a station's docking at runtime.
- Engine AI commands for docking exist (exe): `Carcass:ComeToTradeStation(station)` (used once for the player mothership in `Quests/mission_1/ReturnHero.script`), and `CommandComeToTradeStation` / `LeadToTradeStation` with ini keys `LeadToTradeStationPositioningAccuracy` and `ComeToTradeStationHelperType`. Whether NPC carcasses can use `ComeToTradeStation` is not verified.
- `Scripts/include/Stocks.script`: corporate shares item `stocks` ("Stocks of Llanowarian Standard", `Game/Modules.xml`). The engine calls `CostOfStocks(count, level_difficult, base_cost)` for dividends ("#UI_AF_STOCKS_ADD_FINANCE") and `SellCostOfStocks` for the sale price; the formula scales with mission level (exe callback names). This is the only passive-income mechanic.

## 3. Factions, ownership and reputation

- Teams: `Scripts/InitTeamScript.script` (`Table_All_Team`) defines PLAYER, Recruits, Trade, Uft, Pirates, Patrol, Vks, Kfni, Uss, Inoco, Triada, Merc, Hunter, Grey, Templar, Prec, Berserk, Alien, Guard and Rebel. Initial relations go through `new_SetAllTeamRelations` (`Scripts/include/DeclareRelations.script`). Values are N, F and W; the C* variants use `RELATION_POWER_FIXED`. Trade starts neutral to everyone except Pirates (war) and Berserk (war). Pirates are at war with Trade, Uft, Patrol, Vks, Kfni, Uss, Inoco, Hunter, Grey, Guard and Rebel. Vks and Kfni are at war with each other (the civil war). Rebel is at war with Pirates and Vks.
- Per-system groups: every `activate.script` creates groups such as `Trade_Group_1`, `VksGroup_1` and `PiratesGroup_1` and seeds them with `SetGroupRelation(group, PLAYER_GROUP, GetTeamRelation(team, PLAYER_TEAM))`. Group relations change in the engine during combat. On exit, `TeleporterAction` in `functions.script` writes them back with `SetInitTeamRelation(team, PLAYER_TEAM, GetGroupRelation(group, PLAYER_GROUP))`. Coverage is uneven: Pirates in 61 systems, Vks 31, Kfni 27, Patrol 26, Trade 18 (17 of the 80 trader systems), Triada 17, Inoco 16, Uss 14.
- System ownership: `Location_SideList` in `FloodTradeStations.script` `InitShopList()` maps each system to Vks, Kfni, Triada, Uss, Inoco or Neutral. `GetLocationTeam(loc)` reads it; it decides SOS response faction, patrol fines, patrol-station dialog, bounty exemptions and `FillCargo`. Story progress changes ownership only through quest labels read when the table is built: `HafaLife=="Vks"` moves Hafa to Vks, and `Transfer_To_NI` step1/2/3 moves Moscowia, ND, Solncevka, Endoria2, Aurora, Corrino, Shula and Taira to Kfni. There is no other territory-change mechanism.
- Reputation reset: `CreateRelationStationDocking` → `DialogC1..8` → `_temp_change(team)`. It sets the team to neutral for 600k to 950k cr (Trade/"FTU" costs 950000; stations 1 and 5 are free) and also neutralises Patrol. It is gated by quest labels `*_AllowChange`.
- Event hooks for relations: `NewTrigger("reps", cond, func, group1, group2, relation)` (`_EVENT_GROUPRELATION`) fires when a relation reaches a value. It is used in quests (`Quests/mission_6c_1/Convoy1_NJ2.script` `trig_AttackConvoy`) and in `m18c_escort`.

## 4. Portals and system transitions

- Graph: `Game/GlobalMap.xml` has 110 `LocationRecord` entries (name and map pixel position) and 127 `LocationPath` (`way_a`/`way_b`) edges. 263 `CreateManagedPortal("Portal", "<dest>", pos, dir)` calls across `location.script` files. `Portal_inner` portals are in-system jumps (for example Gredos `PORTAL_1/2`).
- Player jump: `functions.script` `PORTAL_X_Condition` (`portal:IsEnabled()`), `PORTAL_X_Action` (confirm msgbox), `OnPortalStart_PORTAL_X` (FX, `Teleporter` trigger), then `TeleporterAction` (relation write-back, `TeleportToLocation(dest)`). On arrival, `activate.script` reads `GetLastLocation()` and calls `IncommingAnim(portal, mothership)` (`Scripts/include/TeleportingStuff.script`). Variants: `RandIncommingAnim`, `Precursor_IncommingAnim`, `AlienPortal_IncommingAnim`. `Long_Jump`/`CustomTeleportate(from, to, movie)` handles scripted long jumps. `EnablePortals(a, b, bool)` opens or closes a link (15 quest uses; saved in `STREAMID_PortalManager`, exe). `CreateRoute(...)`/`ClearRoute()` draw a route on the galaxy map (93 uses).
- NPC portal use: `Carcass:ComeToPortal(portal)` (engine command, 32 quest files) flies a ship to a portal. Scripts then catch arrival with an `f_object_inside` trigger and `Delete()` the flight; `trigger_inside2portal_Condition` also plays the portal effect. NPC arrival is the reverse: create the flight at `portal:GetEntryPoint(n)` and call `portal:Start(...)` for the effect. Neither side has an engine concept of an NPC transiting.
- `Scripts/AI/PortalInterface.script`: `PORTALDOCK()` etc. return the docking offset `Vector3(0,0,25)` (engine callback).

## 5. Escort and convoy

- Engine: `Flight:Escort(carcass)`, with formation slots computed by the engine callback `escortDispatcher(escorteeSize, maxEscorterSize, index)` (`Scripts/AI/escortDispatcher.script`, 18 slots plus a trailing column). `AI.ini [Escort] escortKeepDistance = 20`, `escortEnemySearchDelay = 0.5`. Formations include `shipFormationConvoy` and `shipFormationColumn`.
- Story convoys (`Quests/mission_6c_1`, `mission_6c_2`, `mission_9c` blockade, `mission_12c` evacuation, among others) are scripted per system. Each location's action script builds its own copy of the convoy (`StartInitMis`), sets an escort with `flight:Escort(mothership)` or makes the player escort, and watches `f_died` and `reps` triggers. It sends the convoy to a portal with `ComeToPortal` and deletes it there (`Cravan_To_NJ3`). Failure and success set quest state, team relations (`SetInitTeamRelation("Uss", PLAYER_TEAM, RELATION_WAR)`) and posts.
- Cross-system follower: `OutMissionEscort_start("funcName")` stores a function name in quest label `OutMissionEscort`. `IncommingAnim` (and the other arrival animations) call `OutMissionEscort_run()` on every portal arrival, which re-creates the escort next to the mothership (`m8_escort`, `m18c_escort` in `OutOfMission.script`). `OutMissionEscort_end()` clears it. This is the only existing mechanism that keeps an NPC with the player across systems.
- Dynamic missions: `Game/DynamicMissions.xml` defines generators for TRANSPORT, KILLENEMY, PATROL and ESCORT with customers, cargo names (shown as `Cargo_03`), enemy sides, escort ship (`Arba`), 30 patrol path templates, price settings (`priceAveragePathPrice`, `priceAveragePathLength`, `enemyThreatBalance`) and a manager block (`newMissionChance 80`, `deleteOldestMissionChance 60`, `maxMissionNum 12`). The exe contains `DynamicMissionEscort::InitTriggerFlightDied` (exe). `Scripts/AI/DynamicMissions.script` only returns one patrol path. No script references `#DM_*` keys, and the English `LocData` has no `DM_*` strings, so the system looks like an unused leftover from Star Wolves 2 (inferred; not tested in game).

## 6. News, mail and logbook

- `Game/News.xml`: 445 static `NewsRecord` entries (name, header loc key, text loc key, image, sound). By prefix: 233 `book*` (logbook), 17 `book_trader1..17` (trader rumours), 121 `news*`, 68 `mail_*`, 6 `mail_torg*` (trade tutorial mails added at new game by `InitTeamScript.script`). Image tags: book, mail, igna, VotNI, Studio66 and Hunting (news channels).
- Posting: `AddPost(name)` and `RemovePost(name)` (1001 `AddPost` calls; 539 of them post `news*` from quest scripts). `Game/Missions.xml` adds news on mission exit (`ExitNewsAdd` by exit code) and at the start (`starting_news`).
- Text is static, keyed in loc files. Runtime parameters exist only for messages and dialogs: `CombineStr(key, value)` (used for credit amounts), `SetReplaceValue` (dialogs), `pilot:Message(key, seconds[, callback])`, `OutputToScreenLog(key, seconds)`, `ShowMsgBox(caption, text, ok, cancel)`. `AddPost` takes a record name, so dynamic news would need pre-authored records or new strings.
- Loc storage: `LocData/English/*.loc` is plaintext `#KEY = text`. Most `LocData/English/Trade/*.loc` values (for example `#L_FT_Replica_*`, `#L_DT1_*`, `#L_SOS_Replica_*`) are stored as hex-encoded or obfuscated strings; only some keys, such as `#UI_FTrader_*`, are plaintext.

## 7. What persists in saves

Engine save streams (exe): `STREAMID_RecruitManager`, `PortalManager`, `QuestManager2`, `TradeSystem`, `LocationManager`, `MusicPlayList`, `GameInterface`, `QuestManager`, `Diplomacy`, `Script`, `MapSelector`, `Map`, `SceneStuff`, `TeamManager`, `GameObjects`, `CameraParams`, `Environment`. `LoadFromStream_LocationGameObjects` and `Script_LoadSave::LoadState: invalid GLOBAL_TABLE id` show that the current location's objects and a Lua global table are serialised.

What scripts treat as persistent
- Quest labels: `SetQuestLabel(key, string)`, `GetQuestLabel(key)` ("" if unset), `ClearQuestLabel`. These are the general cross-system store. Scripts encode numbers and flags as strings in them (`CountPauseQuest` via `tostring/tonumber`, `Trader_Info` bitstring, `OutMissionEscort` function name, `__ShopCorrect`, `*_AllowChange`, `*Permit`, `OnRandomPortal_*`, `Transfer_To_NI`, `HafaLife`). Scripts use quest labels instead of plain Lua globals for cross-system state, and every include re-runs its `__initialize_*()` on load. Lua globals are therefore probably reset per location load and survive only a save/load inside the same location (inferred).
- Team relations through `SetInitTeamRelation` (Diplomacy and TeamManager streams).
- Credits (`Set/Add/Sub/GetPlayerCredits`), experience, pilots, the player inventory (`GetInventory():IsExist/DeleteModule`), posts, quest states (`AddQuest`, `SetQuestState`, `GetQuestState`), the active mission (`QuestStart`, `QuestChoice`, `ClearQuests`).
- Shop stock and prices (TradeSystem stream).
- Portal enabled state (PortalManager stream).
- `AutoSave("#AS_...")` is used in 123 places in quest scripts.

What does not persist (inferred): NPC flights in other systems, which do not exist; per-location random-contact tables and triggers, which `activate.script` rebuilds; the "already met" flag of a trader flight.

## 8. Player-facing economy

- Cargo hold: carcass `<cargoCapacity>` in `Game/Carcasses.xml` (player and NPC motherships: `HM_Queen_*` 1000, `HM_Queen_mk2_*` 2000, `Mothership` 3000, `Arba_t` 4000, `Mothership_Lion*`/`corporate`/`KFNI`/`empire` 4500; trader `Btransport` 5000, `Btransport2` 10000). `CargoExpanderModule CargoExpander1` (`Game/Modules.xml`) adds `capacity_bonus 40` for 45000 cr, big ships only.
- Trade goods: `Cargo_01..Cargo_10` are `QuestObject` modules (mass 20, base cost 800 to 1400, tradeable; descriptions name contents such as heavy machinery, perishables and spare parts but all are generic "Cargo container"). `Barrel_02` and `Uranium` are quest objects that `Trad_CargoType` also uses. Shops do not stock containers; they appear only as loot or trader cargo. Everything else traded is equipment (guns, systems, missiles, fighters, `__All_*` lists).
- Income sources: selling loot and equipment at white or black markets (sell multiplier 0.2 to 0.8 of base by difficulty and shop list), captured-pilot bounties (`CostOfHead`), quest rewards, stock dividends, the paid trader info dialog (5000 cr cost).
- Credit sinks: shop purchases, extortion (`RandomPiratGetMoney`), patrol fines (10 %), reputation reset (600k to 950k), repairs (1000) and AI chips (32k to 450k) at repair stations.
- `AI/SellingCoef.ini`: global buy and sell multipliers for Market, BlackMarket and Mothership (all 1).

## Hooks a traffic system could use

Per-location entry points (scripts the game already runs)
- `Locations/<Name>/activate.script`: runs on every entry; it has an "IN SYSTEM UNUSUAL" section for hand-written code. 114 files, so a hook means 114 edits or one shared call.
- Shared calls already present in almost every `activate.script`, each a single place to patch: `IncommingAnim` / `RandIncommingAnim` / `Precursor_IncommingAnim` / `AlienPortal_IncommingAnim` (`TeleportingStuff.script`, run on arrival; they already call `OutMissionEscort_run()`); `OutMissionActivity(...)` (113 systems; runs `OutMissionActivityRun()` once per entry); `StartRandomContacts(...)` (96 systems); `_Trader_Init(...)`; `CreateTradeStationDocking(...)` (76).
- `functions.script` `TeleporterAction`: runs just before `TeleportToLocation`. `dest_location` holds the target, so this is where departing traffic and the next system could be recorded.
- `GetLastLocation()` and `GetCurrentLocation()` give the previous and current system on arrival.

Timers and events

## Gaps (what does not exist today)

1. No off-screen world. Only the current system exists; NPCs are created on entry and deleted on exit or at portals. No NPC travels between systems, and no ship has an identity, owner, cargo manifest, origin or destination that outlives one visit.
2. No commodity economy. Trade goods are 10 interchangeable container types with fixed base prices. Shops stock equipment generated once at new game, never restock, and price items with fixed coefficients per shop list and difficulty. There is no production, consumption, supply, demand, regional price difference or effect of trader deliveries or losses on stock.
3. Flying-trader shops are 12 shared virtual shops (`trader1..12`) with no link to the trader's cargo or faction.
4. Trader interaction happens once per flight (the 70-unit proximity trigger is not re-armed) and is random: trade window, paid rumour or one-liner. There is no hailing, asking for a destination, hiring as escort, demanding cargo or reporting a crime.
5. The attack response is one fixed ambush (4 faction flights at strength 18 to 20). There is no graded escalation, no witness or bounty system for attacking traders, and only 17 of 80 trader systems keep the Trade relation change after exit.
6. Pirates are at war with Trade at team level, but no script directs pirates at traders. Raids happen only if the engine AI's patrol paths cross. There are no pirate ambushes on trade lanes, blockades or convoy interceptions outside story missions.
7. War has no effect on traffic. Ownership changes only through a few story quest labels; trader spawn rates, routes, cargo and escorts do not depend on war state, piracy level or recent events.
8. Escort and convoy content exists only as hand-written story missions. `DynamicMissions.xml` (transport, escort, patrol, kill) appears unused: no script references, no loc strings.
9. News is static. There is no way to report "convoy X lost in Y" except by pre-authoring `News.xml` records and loc strings and posting them.
10. Persistent state is limited to string quest labels (and Lua globals within one location, not verified). A galaxy-wide traffic state would have to be serialised into quest labels or kept outside the game.
11. Timer and trigger budget: `Timer_Seq` has 6 slots; random contacts allow one spawn per tick. Performance limits for many simultaneous NPC flights in one system are untested (`RespawnProg` caps flights per point at 20 with the comment "lags").
12. No observation tools: no traffic list, no trade-lane overlay on the galaxy map (`CreateRoute` draws only the player's route), no trader scan or cargo inspection UI.

Open questions to verify at runtime: whether Lua globals survive a location change; what `UpdateTradeSystem(TRUE/FALSE)` does; whether player sales add to shop stock; whether NPC carcasses can use `ComeToTradeStation` and dock; whether destroyed traders drop their inventory as pick-up modules; whether several Quests.xml missions can be active at once; whether `DynamicMissions` can be triggered at all.
